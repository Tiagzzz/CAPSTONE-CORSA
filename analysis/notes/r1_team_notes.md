### 1. What Made Oakberry Scalable? ("Replicable by Design")
- **Micro-kiosk origin & logistical standardization**: Founded in Brazil in 2016 by Georgios Frangulis and Renatoery, Oakberry's first kiosk in São Paulo measured only **6 m²**, and management treated product standardization as a logistical and scaling advantage rather than a limitation `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)` | External source: HSM Management (`https://hsmmanagement.com.br/empresa-brasileira-se-globaliza-em-apenas-sete-anos/`).
- **Compact footprint, low unit CAPEX, and lean staffing**: Founder Georgios Frangulis stated to *QSR Magazine* that scalability was a priority "from day one", built on small footprints and simple execution `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)` | External source: QSR Magazine (`https://www.qsrmagazine.com/story/oakberry-puts-acai-on-the-map-worldwide/`).
  - **U.S. store parameters**: Preferred U.S. store size of **~46.5 m² (~500 sq. ft.)** (with broader U.S. franchise materials citing 500–1,500 sq. ft. depending on kiosk vs. shop format), estimated U.S. unit investment of **US$100,000–320,000** (or **US$150,000–320,000** on the U.S. franchise portal), and a staffing model of **1 employee during normal shifts and up to 3 at peak periods** `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)` | External sources: QSR Magazine (`https://www.qsrmagazine.com/story/oakberry-puts-acai-on-the-map-worldwide/`), Oakberry Franchise US (`https://www.oakberryfranchise.com/`, `https://www.oakberryfranquicia.com/learn-more-about-oakberry`, `https://www.oakberry.com/uploads/Oakberry_Framework_US.pdf`).
  - **South Korea store parameters**: Representative 50 m² unit total startup burden of **~KRW 200.18 million** (~US$145k) per Korean franchise disclosure summary `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)` | External source: Jumpo Shop Korean Franchise Disclosure Summary (`https://jumpo.shop/%EC%98%A4%ED%81%AC%EB%B2%A0%EB%A6%AC-%ED%94%84%EB%9E%9C%EC%B0%A8%EC%9D%B4%EC%A6%88-%EC%A0%95%EB%B3%B4%EA%B3%B5%EA%B0%9C%EC%84%9C-%EC%9A%94%EC%95%BD/`).
  - **Indonesia store parameters**: Indonesian franchise portal lists shop investment at **IDR 1.5 billion to IDR 2.5 billion (~CHF 70,000–116,000)** with an estimated **24–36 month payback** for a 25–60 m² footprint `(team: research/internal/2026-10-08-customer-behaviour-extended-data.md)` | External source: Oakberry Indonesia Franchise (`https://oakberry.id/franchise/`).
- **Vertical supply-chain control**: Oakberry used its 2021 Monte Bravo / Kilima equity round (**R$84 million**) and 2022 Green CRA debt issue (**R$50 million**) to verticalize açaí processing and supply-chain infrastructure, ensuring consistent pulp quality across global franchise and corporate stores `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)` | External sources: Exame (`https://exame.com/insight/oakberry-capta-r-325-milhoes-com-btg-para-internacionalizar-ainda-mais-o-acai/p`), OAK Holding S.A. 2024 Financial Statements (`https://www.oakberry.com/pt-BR/uploads/Complete_with_Docusign_OAKHOLDING24DEZ.pdf`).
- **Global scale achieved by 2025**: Reached nearly 100 stores in Brazil between 2016 and 2018 before internationalizing, and by year-end 2025 reported **982 units globally (373 in Brazil and 609 abroad)** across 40+ countries `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)` | External sources: HSM Management (`https://hsmmanagement.com.br/empresa-brasileira-se-globaliza-em-apenas-sete-anos/`), OAK Holding S.A. 2025 Financial Statements in Data Mercantil (`https://datamercantil.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/25-06-2026-Data-Mercantil-certificado.pdf`).

### 2. How Did Oakberry Simplify the Product? ("Customization Without Menu Proliferation")
- **Bounded modular assembly**: Customers build a bowl or smoothie from a **single standardized organic açaí base**, choose a cup size (3 sizes), and layer from predefined/unlimited cold toppings (granola, fruit, nut butters, seeds, protein). Perceived choice is high for the consumer, while back-of-house preparation remains cold-assembly, fast (dispensed from proprietary/standardized machines in under 2 minutes), and teachable without skilled chefs `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)` | External sources: QSR Magazine (`https://www.qsrmagazine.com/story/oakberry-puts-acai-on-the-map-worldwide/`), Oakberry Framework US (`https://www.oakberry.com/uploads/Oakberry_Framework_US.pdf`).
- **Global base + localized toppings**: In Indonesia (Bali), Oakberry kept its standardized imported Brazilian açaí base while sourcing fresh fruit, granola, and toppings locally, adapting to local tropical tastes and reducing import friction on garnishes `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)`, `(team: research/internal/2026-10-14-oakberry-benchmark-corsa-locations.md)` | External source: Syauqi Al Ibrama LinkedIn post (`https://www.linkedin.com/posts/syauqialibrama_bali-jakarta-indonesia-activity-7201762548323393536-fbHX`).
- **Hot-kitchen pasta comparator (Spoleto in Brazil)**: Recognising that cold açaí assembly does not capture hot-pasta operational complexity, Ana benchmarked **Spoleto** (Grupo Trigo, Brazil)—a personalized Italian fast-casual chain with **400+ units** using a modular "choose pasta + sauce + add-ons" assembly line. Even with modularization, Spoleto's traditional format requires **R$635,000 minimum investment and 8–12 employees** (6% royalty, 2% marketing fee), while its compact **Piccolo format starts at R$375,000 and still requires 7 employees** `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)` | External source: Grupo Trigo / Spoleto Franchise (`https://www.grupotrigo.com.br/franquias/spoleto`).

### 4. What Role Did Health, Lifestyle, and Convenience Play?
- **Bridging the gap between fast food and healthy nutrition**: Oakberry positioned açaí not as a niche Brazilian ethnic snack, but as a **global functional superfood** (vegan, organic, antioxidant-rich, no artificial syrup) delivered at **QSR speed**—solving the urban professional's trade-off between eating fast and eating clean `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)` | External source: QSR Magazine (`https://www.qsrmagazine.com/story/oakberry-puts-acai-on-the-map-worldwide/`).
- **Sports & lifestyle ecosystem sponsorship**: Oakberry built global premium brand equity by embedding itself into aspirational sports and active-lifestyle events (Formula 1 Haas team sponsorship, ATP tennis, running clubs, surf events, Coachella) and placing stores directly along fitness/wellness circuits (beach clubs in Canggu, yoga hubs in Ubud, upscale malls and Gangnam/Seocho commuter nodes in Seoul) `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)`, `(team: research/internal/2026-10-14-oakberry-benchmark-research1.md)`.

### 5. What Mistakes or Risks Should CORSA Avoid?
1. **Cash-burn trap from aggressive corporate store & capex expansion**: Despite rapid top-line growth on OAK Holding S.A.'s consolidated statements—net revenue rising from **R$173.2m (2023)** to **R$264.8m (2024)** and **R$441.0m (2025)**—heavy PPE and intangible investments (**R$33.5m in 2023, R$44.1m in 2024, and R$162.0m in 2025**) and corporate overhead pushed operating result from **+R$19.4m (2023)** and **+R$19.3m (2024)** into an operating loss of **-R$11.1m (2025)**, net income from **+R$2.1m (2023)** to **-R$3.1m (2024)** and **-R$88.4m (2025)**, with **negative operating cash flow in all three years (-R$8.8m in 2023, -R$43.2m in 2024, -R$32.1m in 2025)** funded by R$325m BTG equity and R$100m debentures `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)` | External sources: OAK Holding S.A. 2024 Financial Statements (`https://www.oakberry.com/pt-BR/uploads/Complete_with_Docusign_OAKHOLDING24DEZ.pdf`) and 2025 Financial Statements (`https://datamercantil.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/25-06-2026-Data-Mercantil-certificado.pdf`). **Lesson for CORSA**: With a strict CHF 200k ceiling, CORSA cannot absorb corporate-store rollout losses and must reach unit-level cash-flow positivity on Store #1 before any expansion.
2. **50/50 JV deadlock / unsustainability at scale**: Oakberry's initial 50/50 JV in Orlando worked to get a single test kiosk open quickly, but management abandoned it after 3 kiosks because 50/50 co-ownership could not scale without dedicated local franchisor governance and territorial master franchisees `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)` | External source: HSM Management (`https://hsmmanagement.com.br/empresa-brasileira-se-globaliza-em-apenas-sete-anos/`).
3. **Trying to sell franchises before proving unit economics**: Oakberry built nearly 100 domestic stores before going abroad and used pop-up/flagship proof-of-concept before scaling franchises in Korea `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)` (note also Indonesian PP 35/2024 legal requirement of 5 years / profitable track record before registering a franchise, verified in Pillar 2 CSVs).
4. **Assuming Bali expat/tourist demand equals Jakarta daily commuter demand**: Even 3 years after entering Bali (Nov 2023), Oakberry has not yet opened a Jakarta store (`oakberry.id`), proving that prime Jakarta mall/CBD real estate and local office-worker adoption are harder to crack than Bali's tourist circuit `(team: research/internal/2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md)`, `(team: research/internal/2026-10-14-oakberry-benchmark-corsa-locations.md)`.

### 6. Structural Comparison: How Does CORSA Differ from Oakberry?

| Dimension | OAKBERRY (Benchmark) | CORSA (Proposed Concept) | Strategic & Financial Implication for CORSA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brand Heritage & Positioning** | Brazilian organic superfood / surf, wellness & F1/tennis lifestyle (`2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md`) | **Swiss-engineered, Italian racing ("Corsa") heritage** combining Mediterranean culinary craft with functional athletic performance | Cannot rely on an existing "superfood commodity" halo like açaí; must build brand desire around **"Fast Casual, Healthy Fuel"** and Italian racing aesthetics via running/padel community activations. |
| **Core Product & Kitchen Complexity** | **Cold assembly bowl & smoothie** from a single frozen açaí base + cold ambient toppings (`2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md`) | **Freshly cooked hot pasta** + **specialty espresso bar** + **blended functional protein/superfood drinks** | **Higher CAPEX, MEP & labour**: Hot pasta requires pasta cookers/boilers, drainage/grease traps, HVAC/extraction, cold-chain sauces, and multi-station workflow (closer to Spoleto's 7–12 staff than Oakberry's 1–3 staff). |
| **Menu Architecture** | 1 base, 3 cup sizes, unlimited cold toppings ("customization without menu proliferation") | Multi-category menu (pasta mains, specialty coffee, functional shakes/smoothies) | **Risk of menu bloat**: CORSA must adopt Oakberry/Spoleto's bounded modularity—e.g., **2–3 fresh pasta shapes, 4–5 pre-batched signature sauces, 3–4 functional protein/macro add-ons**—to keep ticket times under 3–4 minutes. |
| **Store Footprint & Social Format** | **6 m² to 50 m² grab-and-go kiosk** / counter; minimal seating (`2026-10-18-oakberry-benchmark.md`) | **Sit-down "third place" + takeaway hybrid** (50–100+ m² inline or lifestyle mall unit, or pop-up test first) | Jakarta consumers treat cafés and casual dining as air-conditioned **social/work third places (`nongkrong`)** and lunch destinations; a pure 15 m² standing pasta kiosk faces cultural friction, though a **Seoul-style pop-up or compact 35–50 m² counter in SCBD/Senopati** can serve as Phase 1 proof-of-concept within the CHF 200k budget. |
| **Daypart Coverage** | Primarily mid-morning, post-workout, and afternoon snack/treat | **All-day 3-daypart engine**: Morning coffee/post-run shake $\rightarrow$ Midday office lunch pasta $\rightarrow$ Afternoon/evening coffee, padel/run recovery & casual dinner | Stronger revenue density across dayparts if executed well, but requires distinct operational transitions between barista-led morning rush and kitchen-led lunch/dinner peaks. |
