## 6. Location and district economics

**Why we looked.** A flagship needs demand without occupancy cost eroding contribution margin. The model compares six corridors using 100–200 m² listings; these are asking rents, not signed deals, and ruko area includes all floors (`Rent_Data!A8:T33`; `Location!C8:F13`).

**What the model says.** SCBD has median IDR 408,691/m²/month (n=2), IDR 120,000/m²/month service charge, demand index 1.10, model revenue IDR 809.2m/month and occupancy cost 9.80% (`Location!C8:T8`). ## 7. Entry route, regulation and adviser questions

**PT PMA is the gate.** BKPM requires planned investment above IDR 10bn excluding land/buildings; F&B is framed per two-digit KBLI and location point (`[BKPM Permen 5/2025](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf), E-04; N–na`). Paid-up capital is at least IDR 2.5bn per PT, locked 12 months subject to stated exceptions (`E-03; N–na`). **Analysis:** base lease-mode CAPEX just fits the CHF 200k frame, but that does not prove a standalone PT PMA clears both legal tests. Counsel must classify Jakarta’s location point and whether fit-out, deposits, working capital or loans count.

**Halal is operational.** The applicable phase started 18 October 2026; BPJPH’s 9 October notice said no postponement, while special provisions for micro/small firms and imports require re-checking (`[PP 42/2024](https://jdih.sumutprov.go.id/assets/reg/1735286333884_PP_NOMOR_42_TAHUN_2024.pdf), H-01/H-02; N–na; [BPJPH](https://bpjph.halal.go.id/main/read/wajib-halal-berlaku-18-oktober-2026-bpjph-siapkan-aturan-yang-memudahkan-pelaku-usaha), H-04; N–na; current 18 Oct 2026`). Halal/non-halal locations, tools, storage and serving must be separated (`H-06/H-07; N–na`). Listed large/foreign certification is IDR 12.5m plus IDR 800k foreign-certificate registration; inspection is by category/mandays, not an all-in quote (`[BPJPH Kepkaban 22/2024](https://cmsbl.halal.go.id/uploads/Kepkaban_22_2024_Perubahan_IV_Kepkaban_141_Tahun_2021_9a2ede13b7.pdf), H-08/H-09/H-12; N–na`). **Analysis:** design one auditable halal kitchen and supply chain, not a mixed-kitchen “corner”.

**Routes within CHF 200k are conditional.** (1) Own PT PMA is unsafe without counsel-confirmed relief and counting rules. (2) An Indonesian-owned operator with a CORSA licence/franchise avoids the outlet operator’s PMA threshold, but may be *Waralaba*: STPW applies and PP 35/2024 states three years’ operation plus two audited profitable years; Permendag 71/2019 still shows five years, so OSS practice needs counsel (`[PP 35/2024](https://jdih.kemendag.go.id/peraturan/peraturan-pemerintah-nomor-35-tahun-2024-tentang-waralaba-1), F-02/F-04/F-07; N–na; [Permendag 71/2019](https://jdih.kemendag.go.id/peraturan/peraturan-menteri-perdagangan-nomor-71-tahun-2019-penyelenggaraan-waralaba), F-05; N–na`). Foreign-franchisor documents are specified (`F-03; N–na`); regional STPW is capped at five working days, not central processing (`[Permendag 25/2025](https://jdih.kemendag.go.id/peraturan/peraturan-menteri-perdagangan-republik-indonesia-nomor-25-tahun-2025-tentang-tata-cara-penerbitan-surat-tanda-pendaftaran-waralaba-oleh-pemerintah-daerah-1), F-09; J–na`). (3) A JV depends on whether foreign share makes it PMA and whether a pure trademark/know-how licence falls outside *Waralaba*. **Analysis:** route 2 is the pilot hypothesis, not a legal conclusion.

**Tax questions.** Budget statutory 10% DKI PBJT and confirm treatment of delivery, takeaway, service charges and retail (`[Perda DKI 1/2024](https://bapenda.jakarta.go.id/peraturan-perpajakan/unduh/perda-nomor-1-tahun-2024-tentang-pajak-dan-retribusi-daerah), T-01/T-03; J–na`). A reported 20% relief was March 2026 only, not a 2027 assumption (`T-07; J–na; corrected 18 Oct`). Ask counsel to settle KBLI/location points; counted investment; franchise/licence, STPW and local-goods priority; halal/imports; corporate tax; Swiss royalty/service/dividend withholding; and VAT on intangibles/merchandise (`tasks/2026-10-17-lawyer-and-tax-questions.md`).
