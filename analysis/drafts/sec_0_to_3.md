## 1. Question, evidence base and limits

The question is whether Jakarta is a strategically fit first international flagship for CORSA, and what must be kept, adapted or tested before capital is committed. The evidence base combines processed demand, consumer, competitor, regulatory, environmental and CAGE rows; the Pillar 1 mapping records each row’s geography, age fit and verification status (team: `tasks/2026-10-18-handoff.md`). Only `VERIFIED-OPENED` and `CORRECTED-OPENED` rows, plus pre-verification items marked `CONFIRMED` or `CORRECTED`, are used here (team: `analysis/notes/r2_pillar1_notes.md`).

The evidence is uneven. Many observations are national or urban-Indonesia proxies rather than Jakarta-specific, most have no age split, and there is no verified Jakarta behavioural row specifically covering ages 25–39 (team: `analysis/notes/r2_pillar1_notes.md`). The Jakarta 25–39 counts below are resident population counts, not customer demand or commuter counts. Analysis: treat them as catchment potential, not a sales forecast. Counter-evidence is strongest on affordability and foreign-operator stress: national middle-class contraction, real-wage decline and losses at several listed F&B operators point against an unqualified premium-growth thesis ([Kontan relaying Mandiri Institute](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/jumlah-kelas-menengah-turun-lebih-dalam-di-2025-tertekan-daya-beli-dan-konsumsi), P1C2-01; N-D), ([World Bank](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060926111525068/pdf/P513006-58b80446-5646-4a53-b39c-800d0fa99b73.pdf), P1C2-11; N-D), ([PT MAP Boga Adiperkasa](https://www.mbai.co.id/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/9M-2025-MBA.pdf), Q6-006; N-D).

Analysis: a partner is a commercial and capability choice, not a stated equity-cap necessity. Analysis: Confidence: medium.
- Jakarta is Indonesia’s leading realised-investment province, with IDR 173.6 trillion in H1 2026, but Switzerland was not among the top five PMA origin countries ([Katadata relaying BKPM](https://katadata.co.id/amp/berita/nasional/6a588db0af3cf/realisasi-investasi-semester-i-tembus-rp10106-triliun), S-11; J-D). Analysis: Jakarta has institutional density, but CORSA cannot assume an existing Swiss F&B support ecosystem. Analysis: Confidence: medium.
- Boycott sentiment is documented nationally and in convenience samples: 80.2% approved boycotting Israel-linked products in one online sample, while a separate five-city sample reported 73.1% agreement ([GoodStats relaying Median](https://goodstats.id/article/kenapa-publik-ri-setuju-untuk-boikot-produk-israel-n84Sj), S-04; N-D), ([RMOL relaying PKAEN](https://rmol.id/nusantara/read/2025/05/15/666584/), S-03; P-D). ## 3. CAGE: Switzerland to Jakarta

### Cultural distance

Indonesia and Switzerland differ sharply on Hofstede’s national scores: Indonesia scores 78 versus Switzerland’s 34 on power distance and 5 versus 79 on individualism ([The Culture Factor Group](https://www.theculturefactor.com/country-comparison-tool?countries=indonesia%2Cswitzerland), CAGE-001/CAGE-002; N-D). In urban coffee behaviour, 44% link coffee with gathering and 34% with work/study, while Jakarta running groups use cafés as meeting and breakfast points ([Jakpat Insight](https://insight.jakpat.net/healthy-living-and-coffee-trends-in-2025/), UC-N13/P4W7b-01; N-D), ([The Jakarta Post Weekender](https://weekender.thejakartapost.com/the-weekend-digest/2025/06/04/the-coffee-run-a-third-space-for-every-pace.html), CAGE-016; J-D), ([IKPI](https://ikpi.or.id/ikpi-runner-community-gelar-sunset-run-5k-di-gbk-perkuat-kebugaran-keakraban-anggota/), CAGE-018; J-D). 62% of respondents report eating rice every day ([Jakpat Insight](https://insight.jakpat.net/healthy-living-and-coffee-trends-in-2025/), CAGE-020; N-D).

Analysis: **Transfers:** the coffee-plus-food third place, community rituals and work/study use. **Needs adapting:** hierarchy-sensitive staff management, sweetness/spice/portion cues and halal kitchen design. **Must be tested:** pasta as a weekly routine and the emotional resonance of Italian racing/Swiss identity; daily rice consumption does not by itself prove pasta rejection. No verified Jakarta row establishes those two propositions.

### Administrative distance

A direct PT PMA route carries a high threshold—above IDR 10 billion planned investment and IDR 2.5 billion paid-up capital—despite no foreign-ownership cap found for cafés/restaurants ([JDIH BKPM](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf), E-03/E-04; N-na), ([JDIH BKPM](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/userfiles/documents/2023/10/2021._10_Perpres_Bidang_Usaha_Penanaman_Modal_.pdf), E-01; N-na). Medium-high risk activities use NIB plus a verified Sertifikat Standar ([JDIH BKPM](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/Salinan-PP-Nomor-28-Tahun-2025-.pdf), E-09; N-na). Halal certification and physical separation apply to the product and outlet ([Sumut JDIH PP 42/2024](https://jdih.sumutprov.go.id/assets/reg/1735286333884_PP_NOMOR_42_TAHUN_2024.pdf), H-01/H-06; N-na), ([LPPOM MUI](https://halalmui.org/larangan-di-resto-bersertifikat-halal-untuk-apa/), CAGE-025; N-na).

Analysis: **Transfers:** the standard OSS pathway and an evidence-led operating control system. **Needs adapting:** ownership, halal assurance, imported-ingredient documentation and local tax administration. **Must be tested:** whether a proposed IP/know-how licence avoids franchise treatment. Every franchise party needs STPW, and the texts contain a practical 3+2 versus five-year track-record conflict ([JDIH Kemendag](https://jdih.kemendag.go.id/peraturan/peraturan-pemerintah-nomor-35-tahun-2024-tentang-waralaba-1), F-02/F-04; N-na), ([JDIH Kemendag](https://jdih.kemendag.go.id/peraturan/peraturan-menteri-perdagangan-nomor-71-tahun-2019-penyelenggaraan-waralaba), F-05; N-na).

An Indonesian network reduces, but does not remove, this distance. Analysis: a local operator can hold the operating entity, handle OSS, landlord coordination and PBJT filing, while CORSA retains brand, recipe and audit controls. The network does not transfer halal liability, franchise documentation, imported-input registration or brand risk. These conclusions follow from the allocation of obligations in the cited rules, not from a measured network effect ([JDIH BKPM](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf), E-03/E-04; N-na), ([Bapenda DKI](https://bapenda.jakarta.go.id/peraturan-perpajakan/unduh/perda-nomor-1-tahun-2024-tentang-pajak-dan-retribusi-daerah), T-01; J-D).

### Geographic distance

Zurich–Jakarta is approximately 11,118 km; listed outbound itineraries take 19h10–19h25, Jakarta is UTC+7, Switzerland is UTC+1/UTC+2, and the time gap is five hours in Swiss summer and six in winter ([1flt.com](https://www.1flt.com/routes/lszh-wiii), CAGE-003; P-na), ([Emirates](https://www.emirates.com/english/destinations/zrh/cgk/flights-from-zurich-to-jakarta), CAGE-004; P-na), ([IANA tzdb](https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/asia), CAGE-005/CAGE-006; P-na). Jakarta’s monthly climate normals are 26.8–28.0°C with rainfall up to 359.5 mm/month ([JMA TCC](https://ds.data.jma.go.jp/gmd/tcc/tcc/products/climate/normal/parts/NrmMonth_e.php?stn=96749), CAGE-007; J-na).

Analysis: **Transfers:** the brand can travel, but not the operating cadence. **Needs adapting:** humidity-proof storage, cold-chain reliability, wet-season packaging and local decision rights. **Must be tested:** whether remote Swiss oversight can maintain quality at a five-to-six-hour time difference and whether each candidate site remains accessible during heavy rain. Jakarta resident population is concentrated in East, West and South Jakarta, while micro-catchment footfall remains unverified ([Kemenkes RI](https://eplanning-ditjennakes.kemkes.go.id/indikator/user/rasio_populasi/SKMRSKAB/prov/31), CAGE-010; J-D).
