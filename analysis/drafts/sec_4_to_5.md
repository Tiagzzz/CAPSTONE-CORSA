## 4. Target groups and customer behaviour

**Ranking criteria.** Analysis: groups are ranked by (1) size and concentration in Jakarta, (2) spending power relative to CORSA's intended price ladder, (3) behavioural fit with pasta, coffee, functional drinks and community, (4) reachability through identifiable channels and triggers, and (5) strength and Jakarta-specificity of evidence. No verified row supplies a Jakarta-by-age cut for the full 25–39 target; national and urban-Indonesia findings are therefore labelled proxies, not Jakarta facts.

### 1. Young urban professionals, 25–39 — volume anchor

**Who and where.** South Jakarta has 179,370 residents aged 25–29, 178,390 aged 30–34 and 178,160 aged 35–39; Central Jakarta has 81,880, 81,020 and 81,700 respectively ([Databoks/BPS South Jakarta](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/demographics/statistics/687652ae17582ab/2183-of-south-jakartas-population-in-2024-will-be-aged-0-14-years), P1C1-S02–S04; South Jakarta/2024/A exact; [Databoks/BPS Central Jakarta](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/demographics/statistics/7c056964f536090/the-majority-of-jakarta-pusats-population-in-2024-will-be-of-working-age), P1C1-P02–P04; Central Jakarta/2024/A exact). The Jakarta average employee wage was IDR5.9m per month in August 2025 ([GoodStats/BPS](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/jakarta-jadi-provinsi-dengan-upah-buruh-tertinggi-per-agustus-2025-npvsb), P1C2-23; Jakarta/August 2025/D exact). **Assumption:** office, desk and professional occupations are the practical sub-segment to test; the verified rows give residents and wages, not occupation by district.

**Occasions and triggers.** Analysis: lunch, coffee breaks and delivery are the priority occasions. National proxy data show 74% of Millennials and 67% of Gen Z drank coffee in the previous six months, while fast food was the most-ordered app lunch ([GoodStats/Jakpat beverage and lunch surveys](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/ini-minuman-yang-sering-dikonsumsi-gen-z-dan-milenial-2026-3m1im), P4W9-01a/01b; [GoodStats/Jakpat lunch survey](https://goodstats.id/article/makanan-cepat-saji-tempati-urutan-pertama-untuk-pilihan-menu-makan-siang-ezXmH), P4W9-08; Indonesia/no Jakarta age cut). New-place discovery comes first through friends/family (44%), TikTok food accounts (40%), social advertising (39%) and passing by (35%) ([GoodStats/Jakpat](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/dari-mana-publik-dapat-informasi-soal-tempat-makan-baru-3WlAm), P4W1-R04–R07; eight-city urban Indonesia/no exact age cut). Affordable price (64%), a unique menu (49%) and opening promotions (48%) are stated attractors; only 16% report visiting during a grand opening ([Jakpat](https://insight.jakpat.net/strong-traditional-tastes-in-the-local-fb-market/), UA-N58–N64; eight Java cities/no exact age cut).

**Price and fit.** Average delivery lunch spend is IDR65,130 for Millennials and IDR58,609 for Gen Z ([GoodStats/Jakpat](https://goodstats.id/article/makanan-cepat-saji-tempati-urutan-pertama-untuk-pilihan-menu-makan-siang-ezXmH), P4W9-05/06; Indonesia/2025/C–B age fit). Analysis: a CORSA pasta-plus-drink ticket around IDR110–132k is a considered purchase, not a routine app lunch. Coffee entry, lunch bundles, delivery visibility and a clearly priced functional drink are the adaptation. National proxies cut the other way: 49% of Millennials reported choosing frugality and 66% cooking at home to save ([IDN Research Institute](https://cdn.gnfi.net/goodstats/uploads/publications/files/2025/08/27/indonesia-millennial-and-gen-z-report-2025-vvhOWWB6M6.pdf), P4W9-16/18; urban Indonesia/fieldwork 2024/C age fit). **Test:** intercept 18–28, 29–35 and 36–45 quotas at South/Central Jakarta sites; measure weekly frequency, bundle acceptance, delivery conversion and last-venue choice.

### 2. Affluent upper-middle residents and professionals — premium anchor

**Who and where.** South Jakarta per-capita monthly spending is IDR4.08m versus IDR2.96m for Jakarta overall, and food represents 34.96% of South Jakarta spending ([Databoks/BPS](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/layanan-konsumen-kesehatan/statistik/721e41cd0521a99/pengeluaran-per-kapita-kota-jakarta-selatan), UE-N01; [Databoks/BPS](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/layanan-konsumen-kesehatan/statistik/e5b26141744c322/pengeluaran-per-kapita-dki-jakarta), P1C3-05 and [Databoks/BPS](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/pdb/statistik/303cd526232af5f/pengeluaran-perkapita-sebulan-untuk-makanan-dan-minuman-jadi-kota-jakarta-selatan-2024), P1C1-F03; South Jakarta/2024–25/D proxy). The national upper class is 1.2m people (0.4%), so Jakarta's affluent catchment is not quantified ([Kontan/Mandiri Institute](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/jumlah-kelas-menengah-turun-lebih-dalam-di-2025-tertekan-daya-beli-dan-konsumsi), P1C2-03; Indonesia/2025/D proxy). **Assumption:** managers, business owners and senior professionals in South Jakarta residential, mall and office catchments are the operational definition; occupation and class distribution by district remain gaps.

**Occasions and channels.** Invitations from friends/family are the leading external factor in mall visits (65%), and dining is the most common mall activity (68%) in a Jabodetabek mall survey ([Databoks/KIC](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/consumer-services/statistics/68786fdf28828/mall-visiting-behavior-of-indonesian-respondents-in-early-2025), UE-N06/P1C3-02; Jabodetabek/2025/D proxy). Analysis: weekend brunch, dinner and social visits are the occasions to validate. Peer reviews matter more than creator reach for high-income consumers in a three-country proxy, and general discovery is led by close contacts ([Marketing-Interactive/YouGov](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/report-54-of-indonesian-beauty-shoppers-trust-peer-reviews-over-influencers), P4W2b-08; [GoodStats/Jakpat](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/dari-mana-publik-dapat-informasi-soal-tempat-makan-baru-3WlAm), P4W1-R04; proxy/no exact Jakarta age cut).

**Price and fit.** Jakarta references span IDR100–250k for Gior main dishes, IDR155–380k for Bastian's European-modern range and IDR100–200k per person at SaladStop! ([detikFood](https://food.detik.com/resto-dan-kafe/d-8181724/5-resto-italia-lezat-di-jakarta-buat-pilihan-makan-siang-spesial), P3PB-26/27; Jakarta/no age split; [Liputan6](https://www.liputan6.com/lifestyle/read/6173407/jelajahi-11-tempat-makan-enak-di-foodcourt-senayan-city-kuliner-lokal-hingga-internasional), P3PB-59; Senayan City/no age split). Analysis: this group can anchor a 89–105k pasta and premium coffee, but should not be treated as a volume base. CORSA's Italian heritage, service and quality fit; halal assurance, local relevance and proof of repeat value must be adapted. A contrary signal is that 77% in an eight-city Java survey preferred local culinary businesses (cuisine preference, not a foreign-brand rejection) ([Jakpat](https://insight.jakpat.net/strong-traditional-tastes-in-the-local-fb-market/), UA-N66; Java/2025/no age split). **Test:** mall/residential concept cards, review-led versus heritage-led messaging, and willingness-to-pay with repeat-visit intent.

### 3. Run and padel communities — occasion and acquisition engine

**Who and where.** DKI Jakarta had 326 of Indonesia's 947 padel courts at end-2025, while South Jakarta had 34 operating clubs and 61 preparing ([Databoks/Core & Court](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/sport/statistics/699fdb88687d7/jakarta-the-indonesian-province-with-the-most-padel-courts-in-2025), P4W10-01/02; DKI/Indonesia/end-2025/D; [Databoks/KIC](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/sport/statistics/699fe8353db5d/padel-clubs-spread-in-jakarta-most-in-south-jakarta), P4W10-05/06; South Jakarta/2025/D). A platform sample reports 58.3% of participation aged 21–40 and its largest segment 31–40 ([Kontan/Liga.Tennis](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/industri-olahraga-raket-kian-berkembang-segmen-padel-catat-pertumbuhan-signifikan), P4W10-10; national operator sample/2025–26/C proxy). Income is not reported; **Assumption:** court users may over-index affluent, but this must be tested.

**Occasions and price.** The reported padel visit is about IDR400k for court rental plus IDR50k consumption, and DKI hourly rental averages IDR423,354 ([Kontan/Liga.Tennis](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/industri-olahraga-raket-kian-berkembang-segmen-padel-catat-pertumbuhan-signifikan), P4W10-11; national operator sample/no age split; [Databoks/KIC](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/sport/statistics/69957685c7ceb/this-is-the-province-with-the-most-expensive-average-padel-court-rental-costs-in-2025-with-bali-ranking-at-the-top), P4W10-07; DKI/2025/D). Analysis: post-session drinks, portable protein/smoothies and group bundles fit better than a full pasta main. Burgreens lists protein shakes at IDR52k and smoothies at IDR45–47k ([Burgreens](https://www.burgreens.com/files/BUKU_MENU_BURGREENS.pdf), P3PR-013–018; Jakarta outlets/no age split). **Test:** organiser interviews, two event pilots, sales per participant, morning trading and partner-venue conversion. Permit risk is material: the verified Jakarta evidence reports 185 of 397 sites lacking a building permit and two North Jakarta courts sealed ([CNA Indonesia](https://www.cna.id/indonesia/lapangan-padel-jakarta-tak-berizin-disegel), P4W5-08; [Metro TV](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/ba4CPmyw-dua-lapangan-padel-tak-berizin-di-ancol-dan-penjaringan-disegel), P4W5-08; Jakarta/March 2026/D).

### 4. Social/experience-led café users, mainly Gen Z and younger Millennials — design overlay made explicit

Analysis: This is distinct from the office and affluent groups because the buying job is social time, self-reward and comfortable dwell, not occupation or income. Coffee is used to gather with friends (44%) or focus on work/study (34%), and 49.1% report café visits as self-reward ([Jakpat](https://insight.jakpat.net/healthy-living-and-coffee-trends-in-2025/), UC-N13/P4W7b-01; [GoodStats/Kedai Kopi](https://goodstats.id/article/hampir-50-publik-ri-pilih-nongkrong-di-kafe-sebagai-self-reward-2HFQJ), P4W7b-13; Indonesia/no exact Jakarta age cut). Discovery can be social proof, but only 7% identify as early adopters and 52% as need-based shoppers ([YouGov](https://yougov.com/articles/50874-beyond-the-purchase-understanding-the-indonesian-consumers-shopping-personality), P4W3-04/08; urban Indonesia/2024/C–D proxy). Analysis: lifestyle café-goers are therefore an overlay in the first three groups, but naming them separately makes the design requirements testable.

Analysis: Price tolerance follows the person's primary group. **Assumption:** an IDR30–50k coffee/functional-drink entry is the relevant test, not a full IDR120k lunch. Revisit reasons prioritise tasty menu (83%), comfortable room (78%), service (70%) and cleanliness (64%); aesthetics ranks last (27%) ([GoodStats/Kedai Kopi](https://goodstats.id/article/hampir-50-publik-ri-pilih-nongkrong-di-kafe-sebagai-self-reward-2HFQJ), P4W7b-14; Indonesia/no exact age split). A Jakarta Gen Z study found café environment was not significant for satisfaction ([Jurnal Riset Bisnis dan Manajemen](https://journal.unpas.ac.id/index.php/jrbm/article/view/38365), P4W7b-15; Jakarta/five brands/Gen Z/B age fit). **Adapt:** make taste, service, seating and power access primary; keep racing/visual identity as a memory cue. **Test:** dwell-time, service-speed and return-intent intercepts; compare brand-led and product-led creative.

**Dropped or deferred.** Students are an entry-price role, not a ranked flagship group: 48% of Gen Z spend under IDR10k per cup, 60% use promotions and 54% limit social outings ([Databoks/Jakpat](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/consumer-services/statistics/677cd50372d4a/how-much-do-gen-z-spend-on-coffee-this-survey-reveals), P1C3-01/UE-N03; [IDN Research Institute](https://cdn.gnfi.net/goodstats/uploads/publications/files/2025/08/27/indonesia-millennial-and-gen-z-report-2025-vvhOWWB6M6.pdf), P4W9-14/15; Indonesia/urban proxy), and student counts are unverified. Creators are a channel, not a buyer segment: TikTok food accounts reach 40%, but creator trust trails family/friends ([GoodStats/Jakpat](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/dari-mana-publik-dapat-informasi-soal-tempat-makan-baru-3WlAm), P4W1-R05; urban Indonesia/no age cut). Expats are deferred because no verified Jakarta row sizes or describes them; do not build the case around an unverified foreign-worker estimate.

## 5. Competitors, benchmark and price position

**Map.** Analysis: CORSA faces three layers: value pasta/coffee, mid-market Italian and healthy bowls, and premium experience-led venues. Jakarta pasta references run from IDR20–29k street portions to IDR69–109k Pizza Marzano, IDR74–81k Pepper Lunch and IDR100–250k Gior; Bastian's broader European-modern range is IDR155–380k ([detikFood](https://food.detik.com/info-kuliner/d-7744610/5-pasta-murah-di-jakarta-harganya-mulai-rp-20-000), P3PB-29/32; Jakarta/no age split; [IDN Times](https://www.idntimes.com/food/dining-guide/menu-pizza-marzano-c1c2-01-b9stm-lbb9kf), P3PB-02/09; Jakarta/no age split; [IDN Times](https://www.idntimes.com/food/dining-guide/pepper-lunch-menu-1-00-cqkqh-cw26mv), P3PB-23/25; Jakarta/no age split; [detikFood](https://food.detik.com/resto-dan-kafe/d-8181724/5-resto-italia-lezat-di-jakarta-buat-pilihan-makan-siang-spesial), P3PB-26/27; Jakarta/no age split). Coffee runs from Kopi Tuku IDR10k and Fore IDR27–33k signature drinks to specialty imports from roughly IDR40–80k++ ([Liputan6](https://www.liputan6.com/lifestyle/read/5999756/harga-kopi-gula-aren-di-fore-tuku-tomoro-dan-gerai-kopi-lainnya), P3PB-34/36; South Jakarta/no age split; [IDN Times](https://www.idntimes.com/food/dining-guide/menu-signature-fore-c1c2-01-b9stm-2rb915), P3PB-40/41; Jakarta/no age split; [detikFood](https://food.detik.com/resto-dan-kafe/d-8334297/5-kafe-kopi-hits-asal-luar-negeri-ada-di-jakarta-intip-menu-favoritnya), P3B-11/12/14; Jakarta/no age split). Burgreens supplies a health/functional comparator: bowls IDR50–68k, protein shakes IDR52k and smoothies IDR45–47k ([Burgreens](https://www.burgreens.com/files/BUKU_MENU_BURGREENS.pdf), P3PR-001–024; Jakarta/no age split).

Analysis: CORSA's illustrative IDR82–135k pasta range would sit above value and around/above Pizza Marzano, while an IDR50k latte is above the seven sourced mass-chain latte points but inside the observed specialty-café range. This is directional, not a district percentile: tax/service bases and district splits are inconsistent, and the bowl placeholder is not comparable to the ladder's mixed bowl class. Fieldwork must capture menus and delivery prices in SCBD, Senopati and Kuningan before locking price.

**Competitive signals and risks.** Fore is a strong affordable-specialty counterexample: its audited FY2025 report shows 322 group outlets, including 149 Fore Coffee plus two Fore Donut in Jabodetabek, and IDR1.497tn net sales ([Fore annual report](https://fore.coffee/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/FORE-Financial-Report-FY-2025-Audited.pdf), P3BF-032–035; Indonesia/Jabodetabek/2025/no age split). CHAGEE had 36 Greater Jakarta stores, and Paris Baguette 23 Indonesia stores ([Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/chagee-s-indonesia-playbook-scaling-tea-culture-across-jakarta-and-bali), P3B-02; Greater Jakarta/2026/no age split; [Erajaya](https://erajaya.com/news/paris-baguette-resmi-raih-sertifikasi-halal-untuk-seluruh-gerai-di-indonesia), P3B-01; Indonesia/2026/no age split). Counter-evidence is real: Burger King fell from 147 to 138 stores and Popeyes reported a -20.3% store EBITDA margin ([Restaurant Brands Asia filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/RBA_03022026182049_Finalupload.pdf), P3B-05/06; Indonesia/Q3 FY26/no age split); Starbucks reported 11 openings and 16 closures in H1 2025 ([Investortrust](https://investortrust.id/market/71087/map-boga-mapb-targetkan-buka-40-gerai-starbucks-di-2025-fokus-di-wilayah-ini), P3B-07; Indonesia/H1 2025/no age split). These are operator outcomes, not proof of demand causation.

**Oakberry benchmark and translation.** Oakberry's first São Paulo kiosk was 6m²; a Florida Mall store used a 50/50 OAKBERRY–local-operator structure, and later used territorial master-franchise arrangements ([HSM Management](https://hsmmanagement.com.br/empresa-brasileira-se-globaliza-em-apenas-sete-anos/), R1-C01; Brazil/US/2016–18/no age split). Analysis: the transferable lesson is a small, repeatable unit plus local operating governance, not the claim that a hot kitchen can copy a cold açaí kiosk. Oakberry's South Korea launch used a Hyundai Seoul pop-up from 7 May to 2 June 2024, then a local operator, influencer/digital campaigns and 36 stores in under one year ([TL Creative](https://www.tlcreative.co.kr/en/projects/hyundai-seoul-oakberry-128), R1-C13; South Korea/2024/no age split; [ABF](https://abf.com.br/premio-abf-destaque-internacionalizacao-chilli-beans-doutor-sofa-oakberry/), R1-C14; South Korea/2024–25/no age split). In Indonesia, the official locator shows four Bali locations and none in Jakarta ([Oakberry Indonesia](https://oakberry.id/locations/), R1-C08; Indonesia/2026/no age split); Canggu opened 19 November 2023 and Ubud 26 November 2023 ([Minami Bali Keizai](https://bali.keizai.biz/headline/598/), R1-C09; Bali/2023/no age split). Its Indonesia franchise page states IDR1.5–2.5bn investment and 24–36 months average payback, but does not evidence the previously claimed footprint or CHF conversion ([Oakberry Indonesia franchise](https://oakberry.id/franchise/), R1-C12; Indonesia/2026/no age split).

Oakberry localised Bali toppings and add-ons through local suppliers; the corrected evidence does not establish how the base was sourced ([Syauqi Al Ibrama](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/syauqialibrama_bali-jakarta-indonesia-activity-7201762548323393536-fbHX), R1-C11; Bali/no age split). Health and convenience should be treated as testable value propositions: 65% of Indonesian consumers say healthy nutrition is more important than five years ago, but only 19% would pay 20% more and 43% cite cost as a barrier ([NIQ](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/news-center/2025/niq-indonesian-consumers-reassess-health-priorities-seek-greater-transparency-support-and-smart-solutions/), P4W4-04/02/06; Indonesia/2025/no exact age split). Analysis: CORSA should make the healthy choice fast, legible and affordable rather than rely on a premium health halo. Analysis: CORSA should translate the principle—bounded choice, local ingredients, teachable assembly and a pop-up/partner test—but simplify pasta SKUs, sauce recipes and drink modules before scaling. Analysis: a complementary hot-kitchen benchmark would need a separately verified source; the unconfirmed Spoleto claims are excluded. CORSA differs by combining hot pasta, specialty coffee and functional drinks; that raises labour, speed, food-safety and halal complexity. **Requirement:** pilot a compact menu and partner-led operating model, validate repeat purchase and service time, and keep master franchising or international replication as a later option—not a day-one assumption.
