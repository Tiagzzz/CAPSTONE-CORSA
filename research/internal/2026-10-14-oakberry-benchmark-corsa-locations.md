**01 Orlando — first international test**

Florida Mall, United States \| July 2018

**Why Orlando, and why this location?**

**OAKBERRY had originally considered the U.S. for its launch**, but started in Brazil in 2016. Its first overseas store was located in **Florida Mall**, an established Orlando retail destination. Management described the opening as a test.

The business was initially structured with 50% ownership by OAKBERRY and 50% by a local operator. The arrangement divided operating responsibility. After opening additional kiosks in Florida, the company shifted toward local leadership and territorial master-franchise partners.

**The Brazilian market connection — useful context, not confirmed causation**

| **2022 Orlando indicator** | **Evidence**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **33,437**                 | Brazilian-born residents (American Community Survey, 2022).                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **4th-largest**            | Brazilian-born population among large U.S. regions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Florida Mall**           | One of Central Florida’s major shopping attractions, drawing both domestic and international visitors. Its proximity to Orlando International Airport and major theme parks provides exposure to tourists beyond the local population, including Brazilian visitors. |

These facts offered a potentially familiar audience for a Brazilian-origin food concept.

**02 Indonesia — the first locations**

Bali \| Canggu and Ubud, November 2023

The first Indonesian openings were in Canggu (19 November) and Ubud (26 November 2023). Both connected a healthy convenience product with environments where tourism, cafés or wellness activities already shaped customer routines. These are location-fit observations, not a confirmed internal OAKBERRY site-selection scorecard.

**03 South Korea — create desire first**

Seoul \| Pop-up activation, then permanent retail

OAKBERRY entered South Korea through a temporary pop-up at The Hyundai Seoul (7 May–2 June 2024), an established destination for retail discovery. The event operator describes product trials, branded merchandise, multilingual staff and managed visitor flow. It made the first interaction with the brand an experience rather than a conventional store opening. \[9\]

The first standalone street store followed on 19 June 2025 at Apro Square near Sinnonhyeon Station, after the brand had already appeared in department stores and malls. ABF also records influencer marketing, digital campaigns and collaboration with a local entrepreneur as features of the Korean expansion. \[10\]\[11\]
