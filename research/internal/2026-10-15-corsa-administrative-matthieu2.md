**01 ADMINISTRATIVE \| CAGE FRAMEWORK**

CORSA Jakarta Administrative

Halal requirements affect sourcing and restaurant operations. CORSA can retain a broad lifestyle identity, but its menu claims and preparation arrangements must match the applicable certification rules.

# When do the requirements apply?

[Figure 1: image embedded in the source DOCX]

BPJPH reaffirmed on 9 October 2026 that the 18 October phase would proceed without postponement. Special provisions for certain micro/small businesses and imports were still being prepared; they are not an established exemption for CORSA.

| **Question**                                         | **Answer for launch planning**                                                                                                             |
|------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Does every business start in 2026?                   | No. Covered medium and large domestic businesses entered the mandatory phase in 2024.                                                      |
| Does a small first outlet qualify as micro or small? | Not automatically. Confirm the Indonesian business classification and route.                                                               |
| Are restaurants included?                            | Yes. The USDA report includes food and beverage preparation in restaurants and cafés. Ingredients alone do not certify the outlet process. |

**02 IMPLICATIONS FOR CORSA**

Can a small halal area solve the issue?

A kitchen corner is not a demonstrated compliance solution. LPPOM states that certified restaurant facilities must be dedicated to halal production, including relevant storage. Shared equipment and contact with non-halal products require more than a label on one preparation area.

[Figure 2: image embedded in the source DOCX]

| **Operating choice**             | **What it means**                                                                                                                |
|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Halal-certified restaurant offer | Plan compatible ingredients, dedicated facilities and documented controls across the process.                                    |
| Mixed menu with a halal corner   | Do not claim this is certifiable. Obtain a written assessment of the exact premises, storage, equipment and certification scope. |
| Non-halal products               | BPJPH permits their trade with clear non-halal information under applicable rules. This does not allow unsupported halal claims. |

**Sources** [LPPOM dedicated restaurant facilities](https://halalmui.org/larangan-di-resto-bersertifikat-halal-untuk-apa/) • [BPJPH non-halal trade and foreign certificates](https://bpjph.halal.go.id/read/penegasan-bpjph-di-sidang-wto-dari-produk-nonhalal-dapat-diimpor-hingga-skema-registrasi-halal-luar-negeri) • [BPJPH restaurant outlet controls](https://bpjph.halal.go.id/main/read/jelang-wajib-halal-bpjph-ingatkan-jaringan-restoran-jaga-konsistensi-jph-di-setiap-outlet)
