CORSA Jakarta Economic

The evidence supports testing a healthier café offer, but it does not establish a viable selling price. Price acceptance and repeat purchases must be measured among reachable Jakarta customers.

# Will customers pay more for healthier food?

[Figure 1: image embedded in the source DOCX]

NIQ 2025 • Indonesian adult grocery shoppers. Stated willingness to pay for nutritionally enhanced food; separate indicators, not mutually exclusive groups. Indonesia sample size is not stated in the release.

These figures cannot set a restaurant markup. The questions concern food products and healthier living, not CORSA meals. Use them to test the value of a specific recipe and portion at a local price.

**Sources** [NIQ Indonesian health priorities 2025](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/news-center/2025/niq-indonesian-consumers-reassess-health-priorities-seek-greater-transparency-support-and-smart-solutions/)

**02 IMPLICATIONS FOR CORSA**

Which groups should be tested first?

[Figure 2: image embedded in the source DOCX]

Tiago’s team assessment • judgement scores out of 30, not market shares or survey demand. The groups overlap; do not add them as separate customer pools.

Prioritise professionals and residents who already buy café meals, with running groups as one way to reach them. Students and expats can be included, but age, occupation or nationality does not establish a premium budget.

Source: Tiago Reimann, Customer Behaviour Report and Extended Data, pp 7–8; Paul, Report 1, pp 5–6. These are supplied team files, without public links.

# How does a community become revenue?

[Figure 3: image embedded in the source DOCX]

| **Measure in two trial events**           | **Decision it supports**                             |
|-------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Attendance and proportion purchasing      | Does participation lead to paid demand?              |
| Actual spend and product cost             | Does the basket generate enough contribution?        |
| Return visits outside events or discounts | Is demand recurring rather than promotion-dependent? |
| Seat time and additional orders           | Can longer social visits support revenue per seat?   |

Comparable concepts confirm competition, not an available profit pool. Add halal compliance and supplier costs only after quotations. Team B needs paid transactions and repeat behaviour before using a sales forecast.

Sources: Tiago, Extended Data, pp 6–8 and 10, for the trial proposal; financial interpretation by the CORSA team.

**Sources** [BORDER café visit study](https://border.upnjatim.ac.id/index.php/border/article/download/782/86/1106) • [USDA halal costs and process context](https://apps.fas.usda.gov/newgainapi/api/Report/DownloadReportByFileName?fileName=Indonesia%27s+Expanding+Halal+Standards+with+Trade+Impacts+on+the+Horizon_Jakarta_Indonesia_ID2025-0035)
