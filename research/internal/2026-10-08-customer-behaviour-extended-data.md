# 1 Goal of the research and limitations

**Goal.** Identify which Jakarta customers CORSA should target first, and what their behaviour means for **price, product, marketing and the Jakarta financial model**. This answers the scope question "Who are the most relevant CORSA customers in Jakarta?" and the request from Meeting 4 to structure the customer research by category and link it to the financial model and the other teams' work.<sup>\[1\]</sup>

**Method.** Desk research on Jakarta (DKI Jakarta province), compared with Switzerland. **PESTEL** is a checklist of outside forces (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal); we use the **Social and Economic** parts. **CAGE** measures the distance between two countries (Cultural, Administrative, Geographic, Economic); we use the **Cultural and Economic** parts. Sources: Statistics Indonesia (BPS), Bank Indonesia (BI), the Ministry of Health, survey and research firms active in Asia (Jakpat, NielsenIQ, Momentum Works), Swiss federal statistics and two team datasets (a Statista Indonesia export and **3,061** Jakarta restaurant reviews).
