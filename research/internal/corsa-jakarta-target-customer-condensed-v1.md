## 01 The Bottom Line

### A narrow, wealthy niche can carry CORSA

**WHY WE LOOKED** Scope question: who are the most relevant CORSA customers in Jakarta? Answer first.

Key figures:

| Figure | Description |
|---|---|
| IDR 4.08M | South Jakarta spend per person per month (CHF 190), the highest in Indonesia [2] |
| 48.9% | of Jakarta's 10.72M people are Gen Z or Millennials (about 5.2M) [3] |
| Only 19% | of Indonesians would pay 20% more for food with added health benefits [4] |
| -10.6M | Indonesian middle class: 57.3M (2019) to 46.7M (2025) [5] |

### Five takeaways [2, 8, 10, 11, 13, 16, 18, 33, 39]

1. Core buyer: health-aware young professionals, 25 to 39, around SCBD (Sudirman Central Business District), Senopati, Kuningan.
2. They choose on price (60%) and taste (59%); health is not top 10.
3. South Jakarta spends most in Indonesia; staff costs about 18x below Swiss.
4. Risks: shrinking middle class, mandatory halal, crowded coffee, delivery fees.
5. Discovery runs on friends (44%) and TikTok (40%); delivery is 55% promotion-driven.

### What CORSA should do

1. Model the primary segment in Tier 1 districts: SCBD, Senopati, Kuningan.
2. Use three price levels: entry coffee or snack, core bowl, premium bowl or bundle.
3. Localise: halal, spice, rice or grain option. Keep the brand.
4. Acquire through run and padel clubs; use delivery for discovery.

How to read this. Each page: why, data, meaning, next options. [n] = source on page 11.

### What it means for CORSA

- Jakarta works in a narrow, wealthy micro-catchment, not citywide.
- Win on taste, portion and experience; health is the reason to feel good.
- Price and visit frequency are unknown; test both before a lease.

### How to continue

- a Approve the 90-day field plan on page 10.
- b Brief Finance and the Location team with the ranking on page 9.
- c Ask for a deeper dive on one page first.

Note. No CORSA customer asked yet: willingness to pay and visit frequency are unknown.

## 06 Culture

### Keep the brand, adapt the habits

**WHY WE LOOKED** The 7 Oct Market Entry Meeting asked to make culture felt: does the brand transfer?

Key figures:

| Figure | Description |
|---|---|
| 5 vs 79 | individualism, Indonesia vs Switzerland (Hofstede cultural dimensions index, 0 to 100) [24] |
| ~87% | Muslim in Jakarta (population register 2024: 87.36%; other sources 84 to 87%) [25] |
| 8 in 10 | Indonesians plan to spend more during Ramadan (2025) [66] |
| 78% | of South Jakarta runs are group runs [6] |

### Hofstede cultural dimensions index, 0 to 100, Indonesia vs Switzerland, revised scores [24]

| Dimension | Indonesia | Switzerland |
|---|---|---|
| Individualism | 5 | 79 |
| Power distance | 78 | 34 |
| Indulgence | 38 | 66 |
| Other dimensions: Motivation: achievement | 46 | 70 |
| Other dimensions: Uncertainty avoidance | 48 | 58 |
| Other dimensions: Long-term orientation | 29 | 42 |

[Figure: dumbbell chart of the Hofstede scores above, axis 0 to 100; and a colourful illustration of a group of people sharing food, with palm trees and a mosque silhouette]

**CULTURAL GAP [24]**

- Power distance 78 vs 34: visible status matters.
- Indulgence 38 vs 66: say 'balanced', not 'treat yourself'.

**RELIGION AND SEASONS [13, 66]**

- Halal mandatory for medium and large firms since Oct 2024.
- Ramadan: daytime demand falls, evening and group demand rise.

**KEEP VS ADAPT**

- Keep: racing and Italian identity, specialty coffee, Mediterranean health promise, premium design, community.
- Adapt: halal supply (cheese, stocks, no wine), spice, rice or grain option.
- Adapt: Ramadan hours, group formats.

**TURN COLLECTIVISM INTO A CHANCE [1, 6, 11, 63, 66]**

- Partner with run and padel clubs: 78% of South Jakarta runs are group runs.
- Sell 'model for two' and group bundles.
- Make store and products shareable: friends 44%, TikTok 40% drive trial.
- Launch with creators and community events: early acquisition cost is high.
- Offer family and Ramadan group dinners: households about 3.7 people (North Jakarta).

**NOT YET KNOWN**

- No Indonesian data yet on subscription or loyalty interest: test it.

### What it means for CORSA

- Keep the brand; localise supply chain, spice and staples.
- Halal from day one; group formats may lower acquisition cost.

### How to continue

- a Run two run-club or padel events with tracked codes.
- b Add a survey question on subscription types and meal packs.
- c Budget halal certification and sourcing now.

Note. Revised Hofstede scores (older sources: Indonesia individualism 14) [24]. Household size: North Jakarta figure; no province-wide figure published.
