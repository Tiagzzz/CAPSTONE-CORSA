## Focus checks

### 1. PP 35/2024 revoking PP 42/2007; 3 vs 5 years; Permendag 25/2025
- Exact article: PP 35/2024 **Art. 41** revokes PP 42/2007 ("dicabut dan dinyatakan tidak berlaku"); Art. 42 puts the PP in force on promulgation; signed and promulgated 2 Sep 2024 (LN 2024/188 per JDIH). Confirmed from the official PDF on jdih.kemendag.go.id. Art. 40: STPWs issued earlier remain valid to expiry.
- Conflict: PP 35/2024 Art. 4(5) (3 consecutive years of the franchised business + 2 years of audited profit) versus Permendag 71/2019 Art. 2(3) (at least 5 years of experience). Permendag 71/2019 is still "Berlaku" on JDIH and its record lists only older instruments it revoked.
- Does Permendag 25/2025 resolve it? **No.** Its text (opened) covers only the procedure for regional STPW issuance (Art. 2-4: verification and issue within 5 working days, receipt as provisional proof), has no revoking article (Art. 8 only sets the effective date, 30 days after promulgation), and does not mention Permendag 71/2019 or the 3/5-year test. Its preamble lists PP 35/2024 and PP 28/2025 as legal basis. PP 35/2024 itself contains no reference to Permendag 71/2019 that I could find (OCR text, grep). I did not open Permendag 26/2025 (the plan says it revoked other trade rules, not 71/2019): unconfirmed by me.
- Conclusion for the row text: higher-ranking PP 35/2024 governs by hierarchy (analysis), but formal status of 71/2019 is "Berlaku" and the practical OSS test is unknown. The CSV wording is accurate.

### 2. PBJT 10%, IDR 42m threshold, Kepgub 722/2025 and 310/2026
- 10%: Perda 1/2024 Art. 53(1) and the Bapenda page agree. No amendment found by me or by the worker (JDIH Jakarta status page for the Perda still not found).
- IDR 42,000,000 per month: Art. 45(2)(a) is an exclusion from PBJT for sellers whose monthly turnover (peredaran usaha) does not exceed that amount; Art. 45(3): not for incidental sales. It is a turnover threshold, not profit.
