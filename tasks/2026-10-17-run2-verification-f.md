## 2. Priority block: E-04 and E-05 (investment threshold)

### 2.1 Texts as opened

**Permen Investasi dan Hilirisasi/Kepala BKPM 5/2025**, set 1 Oct 2025 ("Ditetapkan di Jakarta pada tanggal 1 Oktober 2025"), promulgated 2 Oct 2025 ("Diundangkan ... 2 Oktober 2025"), BN 2025 (738) on the JDIH page; Art. 400: "mulai berlaku pada tanggal diundangkan".

- Art. 26(1): "Badan usaha ... yang dikategorikan PMA merupakan usaha besar dan wajib mengikuti ketentuan minimum nilai investasi, kecuali ditentukan lain berdasarkan peraturan perundang-undangan." (A PMA is a large business and must follow the minimum investment rule unless other law provides otherwise.)
- Art. 26(2): "total investasi lebih besar dari Rp10.000.000.000,00 (sepuluh miliar rupiah), di luar tanah dan bangunan per bidang usaha KBLI 5 (lima) digit per lokasi proyek." (Investment above IDR 10bn excluding land and buildings, per 5-digit KBLI per project location.)
- Art. 26(3)(b): "jasa makanan dan minuman, lebih besar dari Rp10.000.000.000,00 (sepuluh miliar rupiah) di luar tanah dan bangunan, adalah per 2 (dua) digit awal KBLI per 1 (satu) titik lokasi" (for food and beverage services: above IDR 10bn excluding land and buildings, per first 2 digits of KBLI per 1 location point).
- Art. 26(4): "Ketentuan Titik lokasi sebagaimana dimaksud pada ayat (3) huruf b berlaku per kabupaten/kota." (The location point applies per regency/city.)
- Art. 26(5): land and building are included for property, short/long-term accommodation, farming, plantation, livestock, aquaculture. Not applicable to restaurants.
- Art. 26(9)-(10): IDR 2,500,000,000 "modal ditempatkan/disetor ... per perseroan terbatas" (paid-up capital per PT, company-level).

**PP 28/2025**, set and promulgated 5 Jun 2025 (LN 2025 (98)); Art. 552 "mulai berlaku pada tanggal diundangkan".
- Art. 212(2): "per bidang usaha KBLI 5 (lima) digit per lokasi usaha harus lebih besar dari Rp10.000.000.000,00 (sepuluh miliar rupiah), di luar tanah dan bangunan."
- Art. 212(3)(b): "jasa makanan dan minuman sepanjang terbuka untuk Penanaman Modal Asing per 2 (dua) digit awal KBLI per 1 (satu) titik lokasi usaha, total investasinya lebih besar dari Rp10.000.000.000,00 (sepuluh miliar rupiah) di luar tanah dan bangunan" (food and beverage services, insofar as open to foreign investment).
- PP 28 Art. 212 has no "kabupaten/kota" definition; it says "titik lokasi usaha". The kabupaten/kota definition is only in Permen Art. 26(4) (a ministerial rule implementing the PP).

### 2.2 Answers to the three questions

**(a) Does the rule apply to KBLI 56101 and 56303?** Yes on the text, by classification. Art. 26(3)(b) and Art. 212(3)(b) name "jasa makanan dan minuman", not a KBLI code. KBLI 2025 (Peraturan BPS 7/2025 booklet) lists 56101 "AKTIVITAS PENYEDIAAN MAKANAN DI BANGUNAN TETAP" (restoran, rumah makan, kantin/kafetaria, restoran cepat saji) and 56303 "AKTIVITAS RUMAH MINUM/KAFE" within division 56 (the food and beverage activity division). Mapping "first 2 digits = 56" to both codes is therefore a sound reading but is an inference; no provision lists the codes. Under that reading 56101 and 56303 in the same kabupaten/kota share one IDR 10bn base (this is the worker's analysis in E-04, correctly labelled as analysis). Nothing in the text excludes cafes. The phrase "sepanjang terbuka untuk Penanaman Modal Asing" (PP Art. 212(3)(b) only) is satisfied for these codes per E-01.

**(b) How is "lokasi" defined?** Three different wordings, none defined in the Definitions article (Permen Art. 1; searched for "Lokasi" definitions and found none): general rule "lokasi proyek" (Permen 26(2)) or "lokasi usaha" (PP 212(2)); F&B rule "titik lokasi", which Permen 26(4) fixes as "per kabupaten/kota". So for F&B the "location" is the regency/city, not the outlet, not the building, not the address. Two outlets in the same kabupaten/kota fall within one point. In Jakarta: DKI Jakarta has administrative cities and one administrative regency (Kepulauan Seribu) but Permen Art. 28(3) says that in DKI the authority of the kabupaten/kota DPMPTSP is exercised by the provincial government. The texts do not say whether an administrative city counts as "kabupaten/kota" for Art. 26(4) (lawyer question Q1).

**(c) Exceptions?** Reading Art. 26 in full and searching the Permen and PP for related provisions:
- Art. 26(3) lists only wholesale (per 4 digits), F&B (per 2 digits per point), construction (per 4 digits), industry (per production line). Each keeps IDR 10bn and changes only the counting base. None lowers the amount.
- Art. 26(6)-(7) (property units, public EV charging stations) and Art. 26(8) (in KEK, "sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan presiden tentang bidang usaha penanaman modal", i.e. defers to the Perpres) do not touch restaurants outside a special economic zone. A restaurant inside a KEK would be governed by the Perpres rule (not read; Perpres 10/2021 body has no such figure that I located, annex image-only).
- Art. 26(1) "kecuali ditentukan lain berdasarkan peraturan perundang-undangan" is an open door to other statutes; I did not find one for restaurants (not exhaustively searchable: peraturan.go.id and peraturan.bpk.go.id not reachable/blocked).
- SMEs: the SME criteria in Art. 25(3)-(4) are for PMDN only (domestic). Art. 26(1) says every PMA is "usaha besar". No SME carve-out for PMA found.
- Partnership (kemitraan): Permen has a Kemitraan chapter (large business with UMK-M/cooperatives, mandatory or voluntary). It sets partnership duties, not an investment-threshold relief. No provision reduces the IDR 10bn if a local partner or cooperative is involved.
- Franchise: Art. 24(5)(a) and Art. 278 treat a foreign franchisor as an applicant for NIB and a franchise registration (STPW). Neither addresses the threshold. A franchisee that is an Indonesian-owned PT is PMDN (Art. 23(8)-(9)) and the PMA threshold does not apply on the text. Whether a PT with any foreign shareholder is PMA: Art. 1(21) defers PMA to "peraturan perundang-undangan di bidang penanaman modal" (the Investment Law is not in my opened set), and Art. 23(9) only says a legal entity "dapat dikategorikan ke dalam PMDN atau PMA"; Art. 23(12) ties legal protection to the nationality of the owners. No minimum foreign share is stated. (Lawyer question Q3.)
- One location covering several outlets: the text counts per kabupaten/kota, so outlets in the same city share the base; I found no wording for or against a lower aggregation (for example a mall as one "titik"). Open.

### 2.3 Further text the worker did not record (strengthens the deal-breaker flag)

"biaya sewa lahan/gedung" (e). Fit-out of a leased unit is not named in either (b) or (c); see Q4.

## 3. Priority block: H-01 to H-03 (halal obligation dates)

**PP 42/2024**, "Ditetapkan di Jakarta pada tanggal 17 Oktober 2024", "Diundangkan ... 17 Oktober 2024" (Mensesneg); Art. 198 "mulai berlaku pada tanggal diundangkan"; Art. 197 revokes PP 39/2021 (LN 2021/49). Text read from the Sumatera Utara provincial JDIH copy (the original peraturan.go.id file not reachable); the copy carries the Setneg "SALINAN" header and all article numbers cited by the worker match.

- **Art. 160(1):** "Bagi Pelaku Usaha menengah dan besar, penahapan kewajiban bersertifikat halal untuk Produk makanan, minuman, hasil sembelihan, dan jasa penyembelihan dimulai dari tanggal 17 Oktober 2019 sampai dengan tanggal 17 Oktober 2024." (For medium and large businesses the phase runs from 17 Oct 2019 to 17 Oct 2024.)
- **Art. 160(2):** same for micro and small, to "17 Oktober 2026".
- **Art. 160(3):** for imported products the obligation is "ditetapkan oleh Menteri paling lambat tanggal 17 Oktober 2026 setelah mempertimbangkan penyelesaian kerja sama saling pengakuan Sertifikat Halal." Today (2026-10-17) is that deadline day; I found no Minister decision (not exhaustively searchable).
- The regulation text gives only the end of the phase; the statement that obligation applies from 18 Oct 2024 comes from BPJPH: "setelah masa penahapan pertama kewajiban sertifikasi halal berakhir pada 17 Oktober 2024, setelahnya atau mulai 18 Oktober 2024, kewajiban sertifikasi halal diberlakukan" (press page, conference "Kamis (24/10/2024)") and "Terhitung mulai tanggal 18 Oktober 2024, pemberlakuan kewajiban bersertifikat halal ... telah dimulai" (statement "Senin (21/10/2024)"). Both dates are on or after 2024-10-16.
- **Art. 155(1)(b), (3)(b),(g):** services linked to "pengolahan" and "penyajian"; Art. 159(2)(a): first phase = "Produk makanan dan minuman". Art. 157 limits services to those tied to food, drink, medicine or cosmetics. So a restaurant is covered as a food/drink product and as a processing/serving service (H-01).
- **Art. 98(1)-(2):** self-declaration only for "Pelaku Usaha mikro dan kecil" with "Produk tidak berisiko" and simple verified process; the micro/small definition is "sesuai dengan ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan" (not defined in PP 42).
- **Art. 196:** implementing rules of PP 39/2021 stay in force if not conflicting (relevant to H-07, H-08).

**BPJPH statement of 9 Oct 2026** (page "Wajib Halal Berlaku 18 Oktober 2026, BPJPH Siapkan Aturan yang Memudahkan Pelaku Usaha", "8 hari yang lalu" on the page, "Jumat (9/10/2026)" in the text). Verified quotes: "Wajib Halal tetap kita implementasikan mulai 18 Oktober 2026. Tidak ada penundaan." and "pemerintah sedang menyusun ketentuan khusus bagi kondisi tertentu, khususnya bagi produk UMK dan produk impor". Conflict inside the page (rule e): the lead paragraph says the special provisions are for "usaha mikro dan kecil (UMK), serta produk berupa bahan baku dan barang gunaan", while the Kepala BPJPH quote says "produk UMK dan produk impor". The worker's H-04 lists UMK, imports, raw materials and consumer goods, which merges the two; both wordings should be kept. The statement does not address medium/large businesses; it also does not change PP 42/2024, and a press statement cannot amend a PP. A related BPJPH page of 4 Jun 2026 calls the 18 Oct 2026 phase "kelanjutan dari penahapan ... produk usaha menengah dan besar yang dimulai pada Oktober 2024" and extends coverage to micro/small, imports and new product categories.

**H-03 (analysis):** the elements are all sourced (PMA = large, Permen 26(1); self-declare limited to micro/small, PP 42 Art. 98; NIB as halal statement only for low-risk UMK, PP 28 Art. 234(1)-(2); FAQ SEHATI scheme "UMK dengan NIB skala mikro/kecil ... penjualan max Rp15M, maks 1 fasilitas produksi & 1 outlet"). The step "PMA = large therefore also large for halal" is not in any text: PP 42 Art. 98(2) defers the scale test to other laws, and Permen Art. 26(1) says PMA is large for the investment minimum. The worker flagged this (gap G3). Keep as analysis, ask a lawyer/BPJPH (Q8). Practical effect is small: a CHF-scale restaurant that was not micro/small by turnover and capital would be medium or large anyway.

## 6. Questions for an Indonesian lawyer

These are the points the texts cannot settle. Numbering is for hand-over.

Investment threshold (Permen 5/2025 Art. 26; PP 28/2025 Art. 212)
- **Q1. Jakarta "kabupaten/kota".** Art. 26(4) makes the location point "per kabupaten/kota". DKI Jakarta has five administrative cities and one administrative regency under the province, and Art. 28(3) assigns the kabupaten/kota licensing role in DKI to the provincial government. Does OSS treat each administrative city as one location point, or all of DKI as one? Does a second outlet in the same city need no additional IDR 10bn, and does a second city start a new base?
- **Q2. KBLI grouping.** Does "2 digit awal KBLI" for "jasa makanan dan minuman" mean the whole division 56 (so that 56101 restaurant and 56303 cafe in one city share one IDR 10bn base), and does it include 56301 bar, 56304 kedai minuman, and the new 564 intermediation code? Which OSS practice applies when KBLI 2025 codes change?
- **Q3. When is a PT a PMA?** Art. 1(21) defers to investment law and Art. 23(9) says "dapat" PMDN or PMA. Is a PT with a minority foreign shareholder (for example a local family-office majority plus a Swiss minority, or a Swiss holding with a local nominee) a PMA subject to the IDR 10bn test? Is there a share threshold, and what are the nominee and "pinjam nama" risks?
- **Q4. What counts and when it is checked.** Does the IDR 10bn test apply only to the planned value entered in OSS (Art. 37(3)) or also to actual realisation (Art. 364(3)(h) makes a shortfall sanctionable)? Which sanction and what grace? Do fit-out of a leased unit, prepaid rent, pre-opening costs and deposits count as "bangunan" (excluded) or as equipment, other investment or working capital (counted)? What evidence does OSS or the DPMPTSP ask for?
- **Q5. Relief.** Is there any other regulation ("kecuali ditentukan lain", Art. 26(1)) giving relief for a single restaurant, for a restaurant inside a mall, for a KEK location (Art. 26(8) defers to the Perpres) or for a joint venture with a cooperative or MSME partner? Is there a reduced regime for an existing business under the grandfather clause (Perpres 49/2021 Art. II(1)) or in the new Perpres under Keppres 38/2025?
- **Q6. Franchise route.** Confirm that an Indonesian-owned PT franchisee with a Swiss franchisor is outside the PMA test on all points (royalty, equity, control), and what the foreign franchisor must register (Permen Art. 278).
- **Q10b. Hierarchy.** The PP uses "titik lokasi usaha" and the Permen defines it per kabupaten/kota. Is the Permen definition binding in OSS practice, and is there a conflict with a higher-ranking rule?

Licensing and status
- **Q11. Risk level and permits.** What is the official risk level and scale rule for KBLI 56101 and 56303 for a PMA (PP 28/2025 Lampiran I.L)? Are SLHS, the tourism-business certificate (LSPr) and RPTKA all needed? Which fees and real lead times apply in Jakarta?
- **Q12. Perpres replacement.** Has any successor Perpres to 10/2021 been signed, and what is the expected foreign-ownership and grandfathering content? Confirm that no amendment to Permen 5/2025 or PP 28/2025 exists (the JDIH search was not queryable here).

Halal
- **Q7. Partial-outlet scope.** Can a "halal corner" or separated line within one kitchen serving both halal and non-halal products be certified? PP 42/2024 Art. 6(4), 20-22 require place and tool separation including serving; no text covers partial-outlet certification.
- **Q8. Scale for a PT PMA.** Is a PMA (Permen 26(1) "usaha besar") treated as medium/large for halal purposes under PP 42 Art. 98(2) and Art. 160, or do UMK criteria apply on turnover and capital? (Practical: is self-declare ever available to such a company?)
- **Q9. Non-halal declaration and alcohol/pork.** What does "keterangan tidak halal" look like for a restaurant menu (Art. 110(3) decision of the Kepala Badan not found)? Can a certified outlet serve alcohol or pork, or a mixed menu? Is an outlet that serves any haram product outside the certification duty for that product only?
- **Q10. LPH category and fee.** Which Lampiran IV row does the LPH use for a restaurant (row 17 "Penyediaan makanan dan minuman dengan pengolahan" or row 47 "Jasa Pengolahan"), and what is a realistic all-in cost and timeline including halal supervisor and LPH scheduling?
- **Q13. Imported ingredients.** Does the Minister's decision under Art. 160(3) (deadline today, 17 Oct 2026) exist, and do imported Swiss or Italian ingredients used in a restaurant (not packaged retail goods) need registered foreign halal certificates? Mutual-recognition status of Swiss and Italian bodies?
- **Q14. Special provisions.** What are the "ketentuan khusus" announced on 9 Oct 2026 (instrument, date, scope), and do they touch medium/large operators or imported raw materials? Is the 18 Oct 2026 start of the second phase accompanied by a different supervision approach (BPJPH says no sweeping)?
- **Q15. In-force of Kepkaban 78/2023 and 22/2024.** Confirm no later decision replaces them, and that PP 42/2024 Art. 196 preserves them; what happens on conflict (for example the "lokasi" separation wording)?
