## 7. Lessons for `tasks/lessons.md` (only if the owner orders a corrective action)

- A derived analysis must name where each input comes from; copying a planner's range into a report without saying so passes the arithmetic check and fails the source rule (cf. 2026-10-17 "Legal/price worker errors" rule b).
- "No rows exist" is a claim about the whole repository, not about one folder: search `data/processed/*.csv` before writing it.
- A verdict that needs a median must not be supported or opposed by a mean without stating the direction of the bias.
