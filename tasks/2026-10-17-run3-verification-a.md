## Focus findings

**1. Padel units.** Core & Court via Databoks (BEH-091/092/093): 326 courts Jakarta, 947 courts and 293 clubs nationally, 34 clubs operating in South Jakarta. A club has several courts (947/293 about 3.2). Jakarta Post (BEH-097): venues (95 open Greater Jakarta mid-Aug 2025; 133 national and 27 Jakarta in 2024), also multi-court. CNA (BEH-044): the same sentence set uses "397 lapangan padel" (journalist) and "397 bangunan padel" (official's quote, Citata head, DPRD hearing 4 Mar 2026). So 397 cannot be labelled cleanly as buildings or courts; P4W10-01 note should say unit unresolved. Never mix 326 / 397 / 95 venues / 34 clubs.

**2. Delivery GMV growth.** P4W10-22 (+18%, FY2025) is Indonesia, three apps (GrabFood+GoFood+ShopeeFood), Momentum Works estimate via Databoks 20/02/2026, GMV incl. canceled orders. The SEA-wide 18% in SD08 is a different number (QSR/Databoks, US$22.7bn) that happens to be equal; both stay, labelled. Per-app (Databoks, estimates): Grab +16% (US$2.94bn), GoFood +5% (1.98), ShopeeFood +51% (1.47); implied weighted growth about 18.5%, consistent with 18%. GoTo Delivery GTV +8% (Rp41.8 trn) is company-reported and includes logistics and quick commerce (original opened). Momentum Works original not opened (all rows relay).

**3. USDA PSD vs BPS.** Both sides are stored correctly. PSD (grain.pdf, Oct 2026), trade year Jul-Jun, wheat plus flour and products: 2024/25 = 10,452; 2025/26 = 13,023 thousand t. BPS via Databoks, calendar year, HS gandum dan meslin only: 2024 = 11.72 Mt, 2025 = 10.84 Mt (-7.5%). Different period and scope, correctly not reconciled. GAIN Jul-Jan: 7.8 MMT vs 6.0 (+30.4%), consistent with a rising 2025/26 trade year. Defect: cross-row numbers in notes of P1C4-01, -02, -07 are off (see table).

Recommendation: re-point DEM-050/060/061 to the apps.fas.usda.gov original URL and drop the "mirror" caveat.

**5. DKI 202,983.** GoodStats (6 Jan 2026) labels it "restoran dan rumah makan" units, 2024, from BPS Statistik Penyediaan Makanan dan Minuman 2024 (national 2,511,128). The BPS page was unreachable, so the counting definition is unverified. Scale (about 2.5 M units nationally) suggests establishments of all sizes including warung; the old BPS DKI 2020 figure (4,237 restaurants) is on another definition. Treat as an establishment count, not a restaurant or cafe count.
