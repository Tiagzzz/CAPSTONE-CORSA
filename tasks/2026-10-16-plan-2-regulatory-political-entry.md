## 1. Goal, decisions fed, research questions

**Goal.** Establish what a Swiss F&B brand legally must do, and what it costs in time and money, to open one flagship in Jakarta; test whether the team's assumed route (CHF 200k own entity, family-office/local-partner network) is feasible, required or optimal.

**Decisions fed:** (D1) entry mode for the 22 Oct interim presentation and the 90-day roadmap; (D2) whether the "CHF 200k" frame in SCOPE §5/§7 survives; (D3) halal operating model (T16); (D4) inputs to Team B (tax, wage, permit, certification costs).

## 2. Hypotheses (confirming and disconfirming evidence)

| ID | Hypothesis | Confirming evidence | Disconfirming evidence / what would change the entry mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | A single-outlet PT PMA is feasible at about CHF 200k. | Official text shows investment plan threshold is not per outlet, or can be met with a reduced/phased or lighter regime; restaurants fully open; no other capital trigger. | Text confirms IDR 10bn plan per KBLI per location (about CHF 463k) with no single-outlet relief -> own PT PMA infeasible at CHF 200k; move to local partner/licence/JV or raise capital. |
| H2 | A local partner or family-office route is legally required. | Restaurants on a restricted list (domestic-only or partner-conditional), or franchise rule forcing local franchisee. | Restaurants open to 100% foreign ownership and no partner condition -> partner is a commercial choice (access, capital), not a legal requirement; the family-office route is then optional and the dependence risk (KICK 46:06) must be priced. |
| H3 | A licence/franchise to a local operator is the lowest-risk route. | Franchise rules allow foreign franchisor with simple STPW; royalty tax manageable; no halal burden on licensor. | STPW or tax (royalty withholding) makes it costly; halal and food-safety duty stays with the licensor-brand; control/brand-quality loss (qualitative) -> own or JV. |
| H4 | A "halal kitchen corner" in a mixed kitchen is workable (Samuel, M5 10:54). | BPJPH/regulation text allows partial-scope certification of a defined area, or sale of clearly labelled non-halal items. | LPPOM/BPJPH text requires dedicated facilities; alcohol/pork use excludes certification -> fully halal kitchen or non-halal-labelled model; re-price layout (T16). |
| H5 | Regulation is a manageable cost, not a blocker. | All approvals obtainable by an NIB route within weeks; fees small. | Any of: investment threshold, halal facility rule, expatriate limits, pending Perpres replacement (Keppres 38/2025 drafting). |
| H6 | Boycott/nationalism risk to a European brand is low. | Listed operators attribute no or transient sales effect; boycott lists target US/Israel-linked brands only; Swiss/Italian brands not named. | Disclosed sales declines and slower roll-outs are large and lasting; policy signals for local content/"buy local"; unrest hit F&B sales. Brand-origin framing is then an entry-mode input. |
