# P2-A1A3: entry requirements (Q1-Q3) and halal (Q5) for a foreign-owned restaurant/cafe in Jakarta

Collector: P2-A1A3 (run 2). Access date for every source: 2026-10-17. Output rows: `data/processed/2026-10-p2-entry-requirements.csv` (13 E-rows, 12 H-rows). Sources opened with curl (browser user agent); official PDFs were read with pdftotext; scanned PDFs (Perpres 10/2021 annexes, BPJPH Kepkaban 22/2024 and 78/2023) were read by local OCR and the two tariff pages were also checked visually. All PDFs stayed in the scratchpad, none are committed.

## Result in brief

1. **Foreign ownership (Q1):** restaurants and cafes are not on any restricted list; default rule = open to all investors (no cap found). Perpres 10/2021 as amended by 49/2021 is still the operative instrument; a replacement is only at drafting stage (Keppres 38/2025).
2. **Capital (Q2):** PT PMA paid-up capital at least IDR 2.5bn per PT; investment plan above IDR 10bn excluding land and building. **For food and beverage the IDR 10bn applies per 2-digit KBLI per one location point (per kabupaten/kota)**, so it does apply to restaurants. No single-outlet relief found. **DEAL-BREAKER for the CHF 200k own-entity frame** (plan 2 puts IDR 10bn at about CHF 463k; conversion not re-verified).
3. **Licensing (Q3):** NIB plus Sertifikat Standar; risk level for KBLI 56101 at large scale reported as medium-high (tier 3 only; official annex not reachable).
4. **Halal (Q5):** mandatory for restaurants and cafes; for medium/large businesses the obligation has been enforced since 18 Oct 2024; a PT PMA is classed as large (analysis), so there is no lighter route. Non-halal outlets may operate with a visible non-halal declaration. Halal and non-halal location, place and equipment (including serving) must be separated, which does not support a "halal corner" in a mixed kitchen. BPJPH tariff: IDR 5m (medium) / IDR 12.5m (large) plus LPH inspection fee.

## Disconfirming and conflicting evidence recorded

- Plan 2 snippet said the rule is "per KBLI per location"; the text shows for F&B it is per 2-digit KBLI per location point (broader aggregation than the snippet implied) but the IDR 10bn level is unchanged (E-04).
- Search snippets quoted other halal fee ranges (IDR 2-5m, 5-12m, up to 125m); the official tariff table gives IDR 5m (medium) and IDR 12.5m (large) for the BPJPH part, plus a separate LPH fee (H-08, H-09).
- BPJPH statements differ on sanctions (two types in Oct 2024, four in Jun 2026); the PP 42/2024 text maps warning and withdrawal to the certification duty (H-11).
- BPJPH page titles differ on 17 vs 18 Oct 2026; consistent with Art. 160 (phase ends 17 Oct, obligation from 18 Oct) (H-02).
- Plan 2 snippet cited "Art. 27 lock 12 months"; Art. 27(1) in the Salinan cross-refers to "Pasal 26 ayat (6)", apparently a slip for (10) (E-03).
