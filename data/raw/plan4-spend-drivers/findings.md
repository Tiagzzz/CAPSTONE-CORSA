## Hypothesis direction
- Promo drives choice between apps (supports) but AOV falling and cost-of-living trading down (disconfirms that promos/premium raise spend per order). QRIS: usage intensity rising far faster than user count (supports payment friction reduction; no basket-size link found). PayLater: growing, NPF easing, but no spend-uplift evidence.
