Worker: P4-W1 (Haiku). Access date for all sources: 2026-10-16. CSV: `data/processed/plan4-media-by-age.csv` (12 rows).

## Access and method

- Source hosts tested at start of run: `datareportal.com` and `apjii.or.id` both returned `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND` on WebFetch (one attempt each). Per the owner-approved fallback, all figures are taken from WebSearch results only.
- Every figure is `snippet-only (not opened)`. The "snippet" is the text returned by WebSearch, which is partly a search-tool summary, not verbatim page text. Registrar must check each quote against the original before promotion.
- Publication dates come from the URL path where the result gave no explicit date; marked per block.
- Nothing was inferred beyond the literal text returned.

## Headline reading (H1 direction)

- **Disconfirms H1 in the one source that measures discovery channels** (P4-W1-S4): friends/close-people recommendation 44%, TikTok food influencer accounts 40%, social ads 39%, passing by 35%. Friends/WOM leads TikTok creators by 4 points in a single survey.
- **No age split** in that discovery source, so the 25-39 test cannot be run from it.
- **Delivery apps are the older-cohort habit** (P4-W1-S3): GoFood leads Gen X (43%) and Millennials (37%). This partly matches H1's disconfirming condition ("29+ cohorts rely on delivery apps"), but it measures delivery, not discovery.
- **Google Maps** is not measured by any figure found. Gap.
