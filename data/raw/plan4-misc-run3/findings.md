## Hypotheses touched

- H4 (community as acquisition channel; Q5): mixed, leaning "partly supported as scale and age fit, unsupported as purchase channel". Padel is large and concentrated in Jakarta (326 courts of 947, S1/S3), skews 21-40 (58.30%, S5, operator platform data) and venues host cafés/F&B (S2, S6). No source shows run clubs or padel communities driving spend at outside cafés, no sponsorship figure, no income profile, no age split for Jakarta Marathon or Pocari Sweat Run. Disconfirming signal: the only measured F&B number is about Rp50,000 consumption per visit on top of Rp400,000 court spend (S5), captured on-site, so padel F&B is largely captured by the venue. Padel regulatory risk is in existing rows (UC-N07).
- H6 (racing identity; Q8): no new evidence on the target segment. Attendance grew again in 2026 (157,875, S13), but it is a Lombok circuit crowd. No Indonesian age, gender or origin data found.
- H5 (Italian origin; Q8): no Indonesia-specific evidence found. Record as GAP.
- Delivery apps (Q-aov): Indonesia GMV growth is +18% but app-level growth is uneven: ShopeeFood +51%, Grab +16%, GoFood +5% (S11). GoTo Delivery GTV +8% FY2025 and revenue +16% (S12). AOV is described as trending down (regional, existing SD07); no Indonesia AOV or frequency by app found.
