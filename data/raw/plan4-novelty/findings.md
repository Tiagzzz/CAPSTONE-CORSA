Disconfirms/limits: only 7% self-identify as early adopters and 7% trend-driven (YouGov Nov 2024, national, all products); Populix: 14% of men try every new trend, Gen Z explore trends only once every few months and Millennials are more reactive than Gen Z (opposite to the H2 "Gen Z highest" assumption); viral venues in the Jakarta region closed within about a year (RANS, Karen's Diner). Trial vs repeat: no direct evidence either way.
