## Findings by question

### Q6 / boycott durability (company-reported, as quoted by news; Plan 3 owns IDX extraction)
- KFC (FAST): 9M 2025 net loss IDR 239.58bn vs 557.08bn (narrowing), revenue -0.76% yoy; management says the boycott has eased but still hurts some regions; cost cuts: 19 outlets closed, ~400 layoffs (Q6-001 to 004). Mixed: damage persisted into 2025, but pressure is easing.
- Starbucks (MAPB): 9M 2025 loss widened to IDR 108.69bn (from 79.13bn) while sales fell only 2.84% (Q6-005/006). The persistent hit is on profit and expansion, not on a collapse of sales.
- Pizza Hut (PZZA): FY2025 profit IDR 24.75bn after 72.83bn loss, sales +9.06% (Q6-007 to 009). Disconfirms a durable, generalised boycott for this US brand; recovery is credited to efficiency.
- Market view: listed boycott-hit firms rose 36% to 100% YTD by 1 Dec 2025 (Q6-010), a sentiment proxy only.
- Counter-view (search summary, Kontan quoting Kiwoom and Reliance Sekuritas analysts): profit declines were driven more by cost (raw materials) than by the boycott. No CSV row (analyst opinion).
- Generalisation: the evidence sits with US/Israel-linked chains (KFC, Starbucks, Pizza Hut, McDonald's, Unilever). No result showed European or Italian brands boycotted. Absence of evidence only; not tested directly. Malaysia comparator: Starbucks Malaysia revenue -36% (Q6-012).
