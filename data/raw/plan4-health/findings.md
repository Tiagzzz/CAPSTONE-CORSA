## H3 reading (interpretation, not data)
Direction: supports H3 (mixed-to-supporting). Stated concern is high (72%/71% worried about additives and ultra-processed food; 65% say healthy nutrition matters more), but willingness to pay drops from 48% (>10% more) to 19% (20% more), 43% cite high cost and 48% availability as barriers, and 50% are trading down for price. Only 46% report active steps. No observed-purchase (scanner/panel) data and no generation split found.
