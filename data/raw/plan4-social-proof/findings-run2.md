## Hypotheses touched

- **H1 (social discovery is the main first-visit trigger): not supported as "main", partly supported as "a major one".** Existing Jakpat rows show close people (44%) above TikTok food influencers (40%). New evidence: friends and family outrank online reviews and creators on influence (2.42 vs 2.36 vs 1.98 out of 4, six SEA markets, 2026); 57% trust close people for coffee information; reviews or ratings are a reason to try a new place for 42%, below price (64%), unique menu (49%), opening promo (48%) and social media/ads (44%). The dataset has no Jakarta-only or 25-39 cut for any of these.
- **Peer reviews vs influencers:** YouGov (beauty proxy) shows Indonesians trust peer reviews more than influencers (54%), more so with higher income (62% vs 33%). Direction: reviews and ratings presence matters for affluent customers.
- **Micro vs mega:** only a qualitative statement (micro and nano creators lose less trust); no Indonesian conversion comparison found.
- **Live and social commerce:** reach is high (92% routinely watch; more than half bought within six months) but promotion-driven (71% for discounts), purchase rate flat (46% to 44%), affiliate links convert under 40% of 83% openers. Indonesian video commerce grew about 90% in transactions in 2025 (all categories). No food-specific live-commerce figure was found.
- **Queues and crowds:** only qualitative expert commentary (social validation, FOMO); no Indonesian measurement.

## Disconfirming evidence recorded

Friends/family and reviews above creators (P4W2b-11); trust in food-vlogger reviews falling after a sponsorship controversy (P4W2b-21); creator links convert modestly (P4W2b-14); live purchase rate not rising (P4W2b-16); generative AI as an alternative discovery channel (P4W2b-13); mega and celebrity creators still dominate in Indonesia (P4W2b-06 notes).
