## 0. Executive summary

### Decision in one paragraph

Analysis: Jakarta is a plausible first international flagship only as a tightly scoped, locally networked test: the strongest evidence is concentrated in South and Central Jakarta, while national purchasing-power pressure, regulatory complexity and untested site-level conversion make a broad rollout premature. DKI per-capita expenditure was IDR 2.96 million per month in 2025, South Jakarta ranked first among 514 districts at IDR 4.08 million, and DKI prepared-food expenditure reached IDR 524,958 per person in March 2026 ([Databoks relaying BPS](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/layanan-konsumen-kesehatan/statistik/e5b26141744c322/pengeluaran-per-kapita-dki-jakarta), P1C1-F01; J-D), ([Databoks relaying BPS](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/layanan-konsumen-kesehatan/statistik/721e41cd0521a99/pengeluaran-per-kapita-kota-jakarta-selatan), P1C1-F05; J-D), ([CNBC Indonesia relaying BPS](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/news/20261007153057-4-774163/belanja-warga-dki-jakarta-rp-307-per-kapita-bulan-buat-apa-saja), P1C1-E01; J-D). Counter-evidence is material: Indonesia’s middle class fell from 47.9 million in 2024 to 46.7 million in 2025 and medium/high-skilled real wages fell by about 1–2% per year over 2018–2025 ([Kontan relaying Mandiri Institute](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/jumlah-kelas-menengah-turun-lebih-dalam-di-2025-tertekan-daya-beli-dan-konsumsi), P1C2-01; N-D), ([World Bank](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060926111525068/pdf/P513006-58b80446-5646-4a53-b39c-800d0fa99b73.pdf), P1C2-11; N-D).

Analysis: CORSA should enter with an Indonesian operator or licensee, a fully halal operating design, an accessible everyday price ladder, and a 90-day test of catchment, repeat frequency and community conversion. A wholly foreign-owned PT PMA faces an investment plan above IDR 10 billion and paid-up capital of IDR 2.5 billion ([JDIH BKPM](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf), E-04; N-na), ([JDIH BKPM](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf), E-03; N-na).

## 1. Question, evidence base and limits

The question is whether Jakarta is a strategically fit first international flagship for CORSA, and what must be kept, adapted or tested before capital is committed. The evidence base combines processed demand, consumer, competitor, regulatory, environmental and CAGE rows; the Pillar 1 mapping records each row’s geography, age fit and verification status (team: `data/processed/2026-10-pillar1-mapping.csv`; codes in `tasks/2026-10-18-handoff.md`). Only `VERIFIED-OPENED` and `CORRECTED-OPENED` rows, plus pre-verification items marked `CONFIRMED` or `CORRECTED`, are used here (team: `analysis/notes/r2_pillar1_notes.md`).

The evidence is uneven. Many observations are national or urban-Indonesia proxies rather than Jakarta-specific, most have no age split, and there is no verified Jakarta behavioural row specifically covering ages 25–39 (team: `analysis/notes/r2_pillar1_notes.md`). The Jakarta 25–39 counts below are resident population counts, not customer demand or commuter counts. Analysis: treat them as catchment potential, not a sales forecast. Counter-evidence is strongest on affordability and foreign-operator stress: national middle-class contraction, real-wage decline and a 2.8% sales decline at Starbucks licensee MAP Boga (9M 2025; section 5 covers other operators) point against an unqualified premium-growth thesis ([Kontan relaying Mandiri Institute](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/jumlah-kelas-menengah-turun-lebih-dalam-di-2025-tertekan-daya-beli-dan-konsumsi), P1C2-01; N-D), ([World Bank](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060926111525068/pdf/P513006-58b80446-5646-4a53-b39c-800d0fa99b73.pdf), P1C2-11; N-D), ([PT MAP Boga Adiperkasa](https://www.mbai.co.id/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/9M-2025-MBA.pdf), Q6-006; N-D).

Phase 2 should rerun the model extraction on the updated workbook, add Paul’s sources through the same pre-verification process, and replace gaps with primary research rather than backfilling unsupported assumptions (team: `data/processed/2026-10-pillar1-mapping.csv`; codes in `tasks/2026-10-18-handoff.md`).

## 2. PESTEL: Jakarta operating context

### Political

- Foreign ownership is open on paper: restaurants and cafés were not found on the reserved, partnership-required or conditional lists under the reviewed investment regulations ([JDIH BKPM](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/userfiles/documents/2023/10/2021._10_Perpres_Bidang_Usaha_Penanaman_Modal_.pdf), E-01; N-na). Analysis: a partner is a commercial and capability choice, not a stated equity-cap necessity. Analysis: Confidence: medium.
- Jakarta is Indonesia’s leading realised-investment province, with IDR 173.6 trillion (PMA and PMDN combined) in H1 2026, but Switzerland was not among the top five PMA origin countries ([Katadata relaying BKPM](https://katadata.co.id/amp/berita/nasional/6a588db0af3cf/realisasi-investasi-semester-i-tembus-rp1010-t-mayoritas-dari-modal-asing), S-11; J-D). Analysis: Jakarta has institutional density, but CORSA cannot assume an existing Swiss F&B support ecosystem. Analysis: Confidence: medium.
- Boycott sentiment is documented in convenience samples: 80.2% approved boycotting Israel-linked products in one self-selected online sample (n=643), while a separate sample of five boycott-sensitive cities (n=810) found 73.1% agreeing that some party benefits from the boycott (a different question, not boycott support) ([GoodStats relaying Median](https://goodstats.id/article/kenapa-publik-ri-setuju-untuk-boikot-produk-israel-n84Sj), S-04; N-D), ([RMOL relaying PKAEN](https://rmol.id/nusantara/read/2025/05/15/666584/), S-03; P-D). Analysis: this is a reputational watchpoint, not evidence of a direct risk to a Swiss-Italian independent café. Analysis: Confidence: low.

## 3. CAGE: Switzerland to Jakarta

### Cultural distance

Indonesia and Switzerland differ sharply on Hofstede’s national scores: Indonesia scores 78 versus Switzerland’s 34 on power distance and 5 versus 79 on individualism ([The Culture Factor Group](https://www.theculturefactor.com/country-comparison-tool?countries=indonesia%2Cswitzerland), CAGE-001/CAGE-002; N-D). In national online-panel coffee behaviour (Indonesia, no Jakarta or age cut), 44% link coffee with gathering and 34% with work/study, while Jakarta running groups use cafés as meeting and breakfast points ([Jakpat Insight](https://insight.jakpat.net/healthy-living-and-coffee-trends-in-2025/), UC-N13/P4W7b-01; N-D), ([The Jakarta Post Weekender](https://weekender.thejakartapost.com/the-weekend-digest/2025/06/04/the-coffee-run-a-third-space-for-every-pace.html), CAGE-016; J-D), ([IKPI](https://ikpi.or.id/ikpi-runner-community-gelar-sunset-run-5k-di-gbk-perkuat-kebugaran-keakraban-anggota/), CAGE-018; J-D). 62% of respondents (n=601, national online panel) report eating rice every day ([Jakpat Insight](https://insight.jakpat.net/healthy-living-and-coffee-trends-in-2025/), CAGE-020; N-D).

Analysis: **Transfers:** the coffee-plus-food third place, community rituals and work/study use. **Needs adapting:** hierarchy-sensitive staff management, sweetness/spice/portion cues and halal kitchen design. **Must be tested:** pasta as a weekly routine and the emotional resonance of Italian racing/Swiss identity; daily rice consumption does not by itself prove pasta rejection. No verified Jakarta row establishes those two propositions.

### Administrative distance

A direct PT PMA route carries a high threshold—above IDR 10 billion planned investment and IDR 2.5 billion paid-up capital—despite no foreign-ownership cap found for cafés/restaurants ([JDIH BKPM](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf), E-03/E-04; N-na), ([JDIH BKPM](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/userfiles/documents/2023/10/2021._10_Perpres_Bidang_Usaha_Penanaman_Modal_.pdf), E-01; N-na). Medium-high risk activities use NIB plus a verified Sertifikat Standar ([JDIH BKPM](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/Salinan-PP-Nomor-28-Tahun-2025-.pdf), E-09; N-na). Halal certification and physical separation apply to the product and outlet ([Sumut JDIH PP 42/2024](https://jdih.sumutprov.go.id/assets/reg/1735286333884_PP_NOMOR_42_TAHUN_2024.pdf), H-01/H-06; N-na), ([LPPOM MUI](https://halalmui.org/larangan-di-resto-bersertifikat-halal-untuk-apa/), CAGE-025; N-na).

Analysis: **Transfers:** the standard OSS pathway and an evidence-led operating control system. **Needs adapting:** ownership, halal assurance, imported-ingredient documentation and local tax administration. **Must be tested:** whether a proposed IP/know-how licence avoids franchise treatment. Every franchise party needs STPW, and the texts contain a practical 3+2 versus five-year track-record conflict ([JDIH Kemendag](https://jdih.kemendag.go.id/peraturan/peraturan-pemerintah-nomor-35-tahun-2024-tentang-waralaba-1), F-02/F-04; N-na), ([JDIH Kemendag](https://jdih.kemendag.go.id/peraturan/peraturan-menteri-perdagangan-nomor-71-tahun-2019-penyelenggaraan-waralaba), F-05; N-na).

An Indonesian network reduces, but does not remove, this distance. Analysis: a local operator can hold the operating entity, handle OSS, landlord coordination and PBJT filing, while CORSA retains brand, recipe and audit controls. The network does not transfer halal liability, franchise documentation, imported-input registration or brand risk. These conclusions follow from the allocation of obligations in the cited rules, not from a measured network effect ([JDIH BKPM](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf), E-03/E-04; N-na), ([Bapenda DKI](https://bapenda.jakarta.go.id/peraturan-perpajakan/unduh/perda-nomor-1-tahun-2024-tentang-pajak-dan-retribusi-daerah), T-01; J-D).

### Geographic distance

Zurich–Jakarta is approximately 11,118 km; listed outbound itineraries take 19h10–19h25, Jakarta is UTC+7, Switzerland is UTC+1/UTC+2, and the time gap is five hours in Swiss summer and six in winter ([1flt.com](https://www.1flt.com/routes/lszh-wiii), CAGE-003; P-na), ([Emirates](https://www.emirates.com/english/destinations/zrh/cgk/flights-from-zurich-to-jakarta), CAGE-004; P-na), ([IANA tzdb](https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/asia), CAGE-005/CAGE-006; P-na). Jakarta’s monthly climate normals are 26.8–28.0°C with rainfall up to 359.5 mm/month (JMA normals, period not printed) ([JMA TCC](https://ds.data.jma.go.jp/gmd/tcc/tcc/products/climate/normal/parts/NrmMonth_e.php?stn=96749), CAGE-007; J-na).

Analysis: **Transfers:** the brand can travel, but not the operating cadence. **Needs adapting:** humidity-proof storage, cold-chain reliability, wet-season packaging and local decision rights. **Must be tested:** whether remote Swiss oversight can maintain quality at a five-to-six-hour time difference and whether each candidate site remains accessible during heavy rain. Jakarta resident population is concentrated in East, West and South Jakarta, while micro-catchment footfall remains unverified ([Kemenkes RI](https://eplanning-ditjennakes.kemkes.go.id/indikator/user/rasio_populasi/SKMRSKAB/prov/31), CAGE-010; J-D).

## 4. Target groups and customer behaviour

**Ranking criteria.** Analysis: groups are ranked by (1) size and concentration in Jakarta, (2) spending power relative to CORSA's intended price ladder, (3) behavioural fit with pasta, coffee, functional drinks and community, (4) reachability through identifiable channels and triggers, and (5) strength and Jakarta-specificity of evidence. No verified row supplies a Jakarta-by-age cut for the full 25–39 target; national and urban-Indonesia findings are therefore labelled proxies, not Jakarta facts.

### 1. Young urban professionals, 25–39 — volume anchor

**Who and where.** South Jakarta has 179,370 residents aged 25–29, 178,390 aged 30–34 and 178,160 aged 35–39; Central Jakarta has 81,880, 81,020 and 81,700 respectively ([Databoks/BPS South Jakarta](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/demographics/statistics/687652ae17582ab/2183-of-south-jakartas-population-in-2024-will-be-aged-0-14-years), P1C1-S02–P1C1-S04; South Jakarta/2024/A exact; [Databoks/BPS Central Jakarta](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/demographics/statistics/7c056964f536090/the-majority-of-jakarta-pusats-population-in-2024-will-be-of-working-age), P1C1-P02–P1C1-P04; Central Jakarta/2024/A exact). The Jakarta average employee wage was IDR5.9m per month in August 2025 ([GoodStats/BPS](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/jakarta-jadi-provinsi-dengan-upah-buruh-tertinggi-per-agustus-2025-npvsb), P1C2-23; Jakarta/August 2025/D exact). **Assumption:** office, desk and professional occupations are the practical sub-segment to test; the verified rows give residents and wages, not occupation by district.

**Occasions and triggers.** Analysis: lunch, coffee breaks and delivery are the priority occasions. National proxy data show 74% of Millennials and 67% of Gen Z drank coffee in the previous six months, while fast food was the most-ordered app lunch ([GoodStats/Jakpat beverage and lunch surveys](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/ini-minuman-yang-sering-dikonsumsi-gen-z-dan-milenial-2026-3m1im), P4W9-01a/01b; [GoodStats/Jakpat lunch survey](https://goodstats.id/article/makanan-cepat-saji-tempati-urutan-pertama-untuk-pilihan-menu-makan-siang-ezXmH), P4W9-08; Indonesia/no Jakarta age cut). New-place discovery comes first through friends/family (44%), TikTok food accounts (40%), social advertising (39%) and passing by (35%) ([GoodStats/Jakpat](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/dari-mana-publik-dapat-informasi-soal-tempat-makan-baru-3WlAm), P4W1-R04–R07; eight Java cities excluding Jakarta/no exact age cut). Affordable price (64%), a unique menu (49%) and opening promotions (48%) are stated attractors; only 16% report visiting during a grand opening ([Jakpat](https://insight.jakpat.net/strong-traditional-tastes-in-the-local-fb-market/), UA-N58–N64; eight Java cities/no exact age cut).

**Price and fit.** Average delivery lunch spend is IDR65,130 for Millennials and IDR58,609 for Gen Z ([GoodStats/Jakpat](https://goodstats.id/article/makanan-cepat-saji-tempati-urutan-pertama-untuk-pilihan-menu-makan-siang-ezXmH), P4W9-05/06; Indonesia/2025/C–B age fit). Analysis: a CORSA lunch at the model's blended net spend of IDR103,375 per guest (`Menu!H30`; model v0.6; PBJT of 10% is charged on top, `Inputs!F17`) is a considered purchase, not a routine app lunch (illustrative model input, not a market fact). Coffee entry, lunch bundles, delivery visibility and a clearly priced functional drink are the adaptation. National proxies cut the other way: 49% of Millennials reported choosing frugality and 66% cooking at home to save ([IDN Research Institute](https://cdn.gnfi.net/goodstats/uploads/publications/files/2025/08/27/indonesia-millennial-and-gen-z-report-2025-vvhOWWB6M6.pdf), P4W9-16/18; urban Indonesia/fieldwork 2024/C age fit). **Test:** intercept 18–28, 29–35 and 36–45 quotas at South/Central Jakarta sites; measure weekly frequency, bundle acceptance, delivery conversion and last-venue choice.

### 2. Affluent upper-middle residents and professionals — premium anchor

**Who and where.** South Jakarta per-capita monthly spending is IDR4.08m versus IDR2.96m for Jakarta overall, and food represents 34.96% of South Jakarta spending ([Databoks/BPS](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/layanan-konsumen-kesehatan/statistik/721e41cd0521a99/pengeluaran-per-kapita-kota-jakarta-selatan), UE-N01/P1C1-F03; [Databoks/BPS](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/layanan-konsumen-kesehatan/statistik/e5b26141744c322/pengeluaran-per-kapita-dki-jakarta), P1C3-05; South Jakarta/2025/D proxy). **Assumption:** managers, business owners and senior professionals in South Jakarta residential, mall and office catchments are the operational definition; occupation and class distribution by district remain gaps.

**Occasions and channels.** Invitations from friends/family are the leading external factor in mall visits (65%), and dining is the most common mall activity (68%; a second Databoks article on the same survey gives 64%; non-probability sample) in a Jabodetabek mall survey ([Databoks/KIC](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/consumer-services/statistics/68786fdf28828/mall-visiting-behavior-of-indonesian-respondents-in-early-2025), UE-N06/P1C3-02; Jabodetabek/2025/D proxy). Analysis: weekend brunch, dinner and social visits are the occasions to validate. Peer reviews matter more than creator reach for high-income Indonesian beauty shoppers (62% vs 33% for lower income) in a three-country proxy, and general discovery is led by close contacts ([Marketing-Interactive/YouGov](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/report-54-of-indonesian-beauty-shoppers-trust-peer-reviews-over-influencers), P4W2b-08; [GoodStats/Jakpat](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/dari-mana-publik-dapat-informasi-soal-tempat-makan-baru-3WlAm), P4W1-R04; proxy/no exact Jakarta age cut).

**Price and fit.** Jakarta references span IDR100–250k for Gior main dishes, IDR155–380k for Bastian's European-modern range and IDR100–200k per person at SaladStop! ([detikFood](https://food.detik.com/resto-dan-kafe/d-8181724/5-resto-italia-lezat-di-jakarta-buat-pilihan-makan-siang-spesial), P3PB-26/27; Jakarta/no age split; [Liputan6](https://www.liputan6.com/lifestyle/read/6173407/jelajahi-11-tempat-makan-enak-di-foodcourt-senayan-city-kuliner-lokal-hingga-internasional), P3PB-59; Senayan City/no age split). Analysis: this group can anchor a pasta at IDR89,000–105,000 net (`Menu!C7:C9`; model v0.6) and premium coffee, but should not be treated as a volume base. CORSA's Italian heritage, service and quality fit; halal assurance, local relevance and proof of repeat value must be adapted. A contrary signal is that 77% in an eight-city Java survey preferred local culinary businesses (cuisine preference, not a foreign-brand rejection) ([Jakpat](https://insight.jakpat.net/strong-traditional-tastes-in-the-local-fb-market/), UA-N66; Java/2025/no age split). **Test:** mall/residential concept cards, review-led versus heritage-led messaging, and willingness-to-pay with repeat-visit intent.

### 3. Run and padel communities — occasion and acquisition engine

**Who and where.** DKI Jakarta had 326 of Indonesia's 947 padel courts at end-2025, while South Jakarta had 34 operating clubs and 61 more clubs not yet operating (area inferred as South Jakarta from sentence order) ([Databoks/Core & Court](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/sport/statistics/699fdb88687d7/jakarta-the-indonesian-province-with-the-most-padel-courts-in-2025), P4W10-01/02; DKI/Indonesia/end-2025/D; [Databoks/KIC](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/sport/statistics/699fe8353db5d/padel-clubs-spread-in-jakarta-most-in-south-jakarta), P4W10-05/06; South Jakarta/2025/D). Income is not reported; **Assumption:** court users may over-index affluent, but this must be tested.

**Occasions and price.** Liga.Tennis platform users (all racquet sports, not padel-only) report average spend of about IDR400k per session plus IDR50k for consumption, and DKI hourly rental averages IDR423,354 ([Kontan/Liga.Tennis](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/industri-olahraga-raket-kian-berkembang-segmen-padel-catat-pertumbuhan-signifikan), P4W10-11; national operator sample/no age split; [Databoks/KIC](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/sport/statistics/69957685c7ceb/this-is-the-province-with-the-most-expensive-average-padel-court-rental-costs-in-2025-with-bali-ranking-at-the-top), P4W10-07; DKI/2025/D). Analysis: post-session drinks, portable protein/smoothies and group bundles fit better than a full pasta main. Burgreens lists protein shakes at IDR52k and smoothies at IDR45–47k ([Burgreens](https://www.burgreens.com/files/BUKU_MENU_BURGREENS.pdf), P3PR-013–018; Jakarta outlets/no age split). **Test:** organiser interviews, two event pilots, sales per participant, morning trading and partner-venue conversion. Permit risk is material: CNA reports 185 of 397 registered padel courts (unit unclear) without a permit (UC-N07), and two North Jakarta courts were sealed on 4 March 2026 for missing building approval ([CNA Indonesia](https://www.cna.id/indonesia/lapangan-padel-jakarta-tak-berizin-disegel), P4W5-08; [Metro TV](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/ba4CPmyw-dua-lapangan-padel-tak-berizin-di-ancol-dan-penjaringan-disegel), P4W5-08; Jakarta/March 2026/D).

### 4. Social/experience-led café users, mainly Gen Z and younger Millennials — design overlay made explicit

Analysis: This is distinct from the office and affluent groups because the buying job is social time, self-reward and comfortable dwell, not occupation or income. Coffee is used to gather with friends (44%) or focus on work/study (34%), and 49.1% report café visits as self-reward ([Jakpat](https://insight.jakpat.net/healthy-living-and-coffee-trends-in-2025/), UC-N13/P4W7b-01; [GoodStats/Kedai Kopi](https://goodstats.id/article/hampir-50-publik-ri-pilih-nongkrong-di-kafe-sebagai-self-reward-2HFQJ), P4W7b-13; Indonesia/no exact Jakarta age cut). Discovery can be social proof, but only 7% identify as early adopters and 52% as need-based shoppers ([YouGov](https://yougov.com/articles/50874-beyond-the-purchase-understanding-the-indonesian-consumers-shopping-personality), P4W3-04/08; Indonesia (national online adults)/Sep 2024/D). Analysis: lifestyle café-goers are therefore an overlay in the first three groups, but naming them separately makes the design requirements testable.

Analysis: Price tolerance follows the person's primary group. **Assumption:** an IDR30–50k coffee/functional-drink entry is the relevant test, not a full pasta-plus-coffee lunch (pasta IDR82–135k net, `Menu!C7:C12`; model v0.6). Revisit reasons prioritise tasty menu (83%), comfortable room (78%), service (70%) and cleanliness (64%); aesthetics ranks last (27%) ([GoodStats/Kedai Kopi](https://goodstats.id/article/hampir-50-publik-ri-pilih-nongkrong-di-kafe-sebagai-self-reward-2HFQJ), P4W7b-14; Indonesia/no exact age split). A Jakarta Gen Z study found café environment was not significant for satisfaction ([Jurnal Riset Bisnis dan Manajemen](https://journal.unpas.ac.id/index.php/jrbm/article/view/38365), P4W7b-15; Jakarta/five brands/Gen Z/B age fit). **Adapt:** make taste, service, seating and power access primary; keep racing/visual identity as a memory cue. **Test:** dwell-time, service-speed and return-intent intercepts; compare brand-led and product-led creative.

**Dropped or deferred.** Students are an entry-price role, not a ranked flagship group: 48% of Gen Z coffee consumers spend under IDR10k to buy or make a cup, 60% use promotions and 54% limit social outings ([Databoks/Jakpat](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/consumer-services/statistics/677cd50372d4a/how-much-do-gen-z-spend-on-coffee-this-survey-reveals), P1C3-01/UE-N03; [IDN Research Institute](https://cdn.gnfi.net/goodstats/uploads/publications/files/2025/08/27/indonesia-millennial-and-gen-z-report-2025-vvhOWWB6M6.pdf), P4W9-14/15; Indonesia/urban proxy), and student counts are unverified. Creators are a channel, not a buyer segment: TikTok food accounts reach 40%, behind friends/family at 44% as a first source ([GoodStats/Jakpat](https://data.goodstats.id/statistic/dari-mana-publik-dapat-informasi-soal-tempat-makan-baru-3WlAm), P4W1-R05; urban Indonesia/no age cut). Expats are deferred because no verified Jakarta row sizes or describes them; do not build the case around an unverified foreign-worker estimate.

**Competitive signals and risks.** Fore is a strong affordable-specialty counterexample: its audited FY2025 report shows 322 group outlets, including 149 Fore Coffee plus two Fore Donut in Jabodetabek, and IDR1.497tn net sales ([Fore annual report](https://fore.coffee/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/FORE-Financial-Report-FY-2025-Audited.pdf), P3BF-032–035; Indonesia/Jabodetabek/2025/no age split). Analysis: the transferable lesson is a small, repeatable unit plus local operating governance, not the claim that a hot kitchen can copy a cold açaí kiosk. Oakberry's South Korea launch included a Hyundai Seoul pop-up from 7 May to 2 June 2024 ([TL Creative](https://www.tlcreative.co.kr/en/projects/hyundai-seoul-oakberry-128), R1-C13; South Korea/2024/no age split); separately, it worked with a local operator and influencer/digital campaigns and opened 36 stores in under one year ([ABF](https://abf.com.br/premio-abf-destaque-internacionalizacao-chilli-beans-doutor-sofa-oakberry/), R1-C14; South Korea/2024–25/no age split). In Indonesia, the official locator shows four Bali locations and none in Jakarta ([Oakberry Indonesia](https://oakberry.id/locations/), R1-C08; Indonesia/2026/no age split); Canggu opened 19 November 2023 and Ubud 26 November 2023 ([Minami Bali Keizai](https://bali.keizai.biz/headline/598/), R1-C09; Bali/2023/no age split). Its Indonesia franchise page states IDR1.5–2.5bn investment and 24–36 months average payback, but states no store footprint or CHF equivalent ([Oakberry Indonesia franchise](https://oakberry.id/franchise/), R1-C12; Indonesia/2026/no age split).

An Oakberry Indonesia business-development post on LinkedIn (single social-media source, company self-description, anecdotal lead) says Bali toppings and add-ons came from local suppliers across Indonesia; how the base was sourced is not evidenced ([Oakberry Indonesia LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/syauqialibrama_bali-jakarta-indonesia-activity-7201762548323393536-fbHX), R1-C11; tier 4; Bali/no age split). Health and convenience should be treated as testable value propositions: 65% of Indonesian consumers say healthy nutrition is more important than five years ago, but 48% would pay over 10% more for enhanced nutrition, only 19% would pay 20% more, and 43% cite cost as a barrier ([NIQ](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/news-center/2025/niq-indonesian-consumers-reassess-health-priorities-seek-greater-transparency-support-and-smart-solutions/), P4W4-01/02/04/06; Indonesia/2025/no exact age split). Analysis: CORSA should make the healthy choice fast, legible and affordable rather than rely on a premium health halo. Analysis: CORSA should translate the principle—bounded choice, local ingredients, teachable assembly and a pop-up/partner test—but simplify pasta SKUs, sauce recipes and drink modules before scaling. Analysis: a complementary hot-kitchen benchmark would need a separately verified source; the unconfirmed Spoleto claims are excluded. CORSA differs by combining hot pasta, specialty coffee and functional drinks; that raises labour, speed, food-safety and halal complexity. **Requirement:** pilot a compact menu and partner-led operating model, validate repeat purchase and service time, and keep master franchising or international replication as a later option—not a day-one assumption.

## 6. Location and district economics

**Why we looked.** A flagship needs demand without occupancy cost eroding contribution margin. The model compares six corridors using 100–200 m² listings; these are asking rents, not signed deals, and ruko area includes all floors (`Rent_Data!A8:T33`; `Location!C8:F13`).

**What the model says.** SCBD has median IDR 408,691/m²/month (n=2; one rooftop F&B listing and one unverified office-tower reference listing), IDR 120,000/m²/month service charge, demand index 1.10, model revenue IDR 809.2m/month and occupancy cost 9.80% (`Location!C8:T8`). Senopati main road is n=1 and uses a 2.27× side-street fallback, not a measured median (`Location!C9:V9`). Side-street Senopati (n=10) median is IDR 110,938; Kuningan (n=2) IDR 284,091; Kemang (n=12) IDR 145,917; PIK (n=3) IDR 91,146. Model occupancy costs are 2.51%, 7.57%, 3.50% and 1.96% (`Location!C10:T13`).

**Analysis:** SCBD/Senopati suit an office-led premium flagship; side-street Senopati/Kemang are lower-cost lifestyle tests; PIK is a separate Tier-2 catchment; Kuningan is plausible but sample-thin. These are fit hypotheses, not footfall findings. Footfall, office density, customer fit, visibility, competition and access are blank, while the demand index is a judgement input (`Location!H8:N13`). Before signing, count passers-by and seated guests at three comparables per shortlisted district across lunch, evening and weekend, and get two broker deal-rent quotes. Cheap rent with weak conversion is the counter-case. The model's own lowest-cost corridors (PIK 1.96%, Senopati side streets 2.51% occupancy) rest on n=3 and n=10 asking-rent listings and demand indices of 0.95 and 0.9 that are judgement inputs, so low rent is not evidence of higher sales (`Location!R10`, `R13`, `H10`, `H13`; model v0.6).

## 7. Entry route, regulation and adviser questions

**PT PMA is the gate.** BKPM requires planned investment above IDR 10bn excluding land/buildings; F&B is framed per two-digit KBLI and location point (`[BKPM Permen 5/2025](https://jdih-storage.bkpm.go.id/jdih/jdih/2025Permeninvesthil005-.pdf), E-04; N–na`). Paid-up capital is at least IDR 2.5bn per PT, locked 12 months subject to stated exceptions (`E-03; N–na`). **Analysis:** base lease-mode CAPEX just fits the CHF 200k frame, but that does not prove a standalone PT PMA clears both legal tests. Counsel must classify Jakarta’s location point and whether fit-out, deposits, working capital or loans count.

**Halal is operational.** For medium and large food and drink businesses the halal duty has applied since 18 October 2024; the phase for micro/small businesses and expanded categories started 18 October 2026. **Assumption:** CORSA, as a PT PMA operating at that scale, would fall in the medium/large (2024) group; confirm the business-scale classification with counsel. BPJPH’s 9 October notice said no postponement, while special provisions for micro/small firms and imports require re-checking (`[PP 42/2024](https://jdih.sumutprov.go.id/assets/reg/1735286333884_PP_NOMOR_42_TAHUN_2024.pdf), H-01/H-02; N–na; [BPJPH](https://bpjph.halal.go.id/main/read/wajib-halal-berlaku-18-oktober-2026-bpjph-siapkan-aturan-yang-memudahkan-pelaku-usaha), H-04; N–na; current 18 Oct 2026`). Halal/non-halal locations, tools, storage and serving must be separated (`H-06/H-07; N–na`). Listed large/foreign certification is IDR 12.5m (`[BPJPH Kepkaban 22/2024](https://cmsbl.halal.go.id/uploads/Kepkaban_22_2024_Perubahan_IV_Kepkaban_141_Tahun_2021_9a2ede13b7.pdf), H-08/H-09; N–na`). **Analysis:** design one auditable halal kitchen and supply chain, not a mixed-kitchen “corner”.

**Routes within CHF 200k are conditional.** (1) Own PT PMA is unsafe without counsel-confirmed relief and counting rules. Analysis: (2) an Indonesian-owned operator with a CORSA licence/franchise may avoid the outlet operator’s PMA threshold, but may be *Waralaba*: STPW applies and PP 35/2024 states three years’ operation plus two audited profitable years; Permendag 71/2019 still shows five years, so OSS practice needs counsel (`[PP 35/2024](https://jdih.kemendag.go.id/peraturan/peraturan-pemerintah-nomor-35-tahun-2024-tentang-waralaba-1), F-02/F-04/F-07; N–na; [Permendag 71/2019](https://jdih.kemendag.go.id/peraturan/peraturan-menteri-perdagangan-nomor-71-tahun-2019-penyelenggaraan-waralaba), F-05; N–na`). Foreign-franchisor documents are specified (`F-03; N–na`); the five-working-day cap in Permendag 25/2025 covers regional STPW for franchisees of domestic franchises and sub-franchisees only; STPW for a franchisee of a foreign franchise is issued centrally (F-03), so the cap should not be assumed for CORSA (`[Permendag 25/2025](https://jdih.kemendag.go.id/peraturan/peraturan-menteri-perdagangan-republik-indonesia-nomor-25-tahun-2025-tentang-tata-cara-penerbitan-surat-tanda-pendaftaran-waralaba-oleh-pemerintah-daerah-1), F-09; J–na`). (3) A JV depends on whether foreign share makes it PMA and whether a pure trademark/know-how licence falls outside *Waralaba*. **Analysis:** route 2 is the pilot hypothesis, not a legal conclusion.

**Tax questions.** Budget statutory 10% DKI PBJT and confirm treatment of delivery, takeaway, service charges and retail (`[Perda DKI 1/2024](https://bapenda.jakarta.go.id/peraturan-perpajakan/unduh/perda-nomor-1-tahun-2024-tentang-pajak-dan-retribusi-daerah), T-01/T-03; J–na`). A reported 20% relief was March 2026 only, not a 2027 assumption (`T-07; J–na; corrected 18 Oct`). Ask counsel to settle KBLI/location points; counted investment; franchise/licence, STPW and local-goods priority; halal/imports; corporate tax; Swiss royalty/service/dividend withholding; and VAT on intangibles/merchandise (`tasks/2026-10-17-lawyer-and-tax-questions.md`).

## 9. Key risks and facts pointing the other way

**Why we looked.** A Jakarta flagship can look attractive in aggregate while the addressable customer, frequency and foreign-brand evidence point in the opposite direction. The following are verified counter-signals; they are not a forecast of CORSA sales.

**Budgets and real wages.** Indonesia’s middle-class population fell from 47.9 million in 2024 to 46.7 million in 2025, while the aspiring-middle group grew to 142.0 million ([Kontan relaying Mandiri Institute](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/jumlah-kelas-menengah-turun-lebih-dalam-di-2025-tertekan-daya-beli-dan-konsumsi), P1C2-01/P1C2-02; N-D). Middle-class per-capita consumption grew 4.1% in 2025, but its food category grew only 0.9% while non-food grew 6.4% ([Kontan relaying Mandiri Institute](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/jumlah-kelas-menengah-turun-lebih-dalam-di-2025-tertekan-daya-beli-dan-konsumsi), P1C2-08/P1C2-09; N-D). South Jakarta’s per-capita spending was IDR4.08m in 2025, the highest of 514 districts, but its food share fell 3.1 percentage points from 38.08% in 2024 as non-food absorbed more of the increase ([Databoks relaying BPS](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/layanan-konsumen-kesehatan/statistik/721e41cd0521a99/pengeluaran-per-kapita-kota-jakarta-selatan), P1C1-F05, P1C1-F04; South Jakarta/2024–25/D). **Analysis:** CORSA must win a specific occasion and repeat reason; “Jakarta is affluent” is not a sufficient demand case.

The World Bank reports that workers earning middle-class wages fell from 14.5% in 2018 to 7.1% in 2025; median real wages fell about 1% for medium-skilled and 2% for high-skilled workers per year over the same period ([World Bank](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060926111525068/pdf/P513006-58b80446-5646-4a53-b39c-800d0fa99b73.pdf), P1C2-10/P1C2-11; N-D). **Analysis:** this is a direct risk to a white-collar lunch proposition, even though Jakarta’s upper tier may remain resilient.

**Savings and eating-out restraint.** The middle-group Mandiri Saving Index was 100.4 in February 2026, its lowest since October 2023 ([CNBC Indonesia relaying Mandiri Spending Index](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/research/20260317113109-128-719750/kelas-menengah-royal-belanja-jelang-lebaran-tapi-tabungannya-menipis), P1C2-33; N-D). The highest household-expenditure group saved 16.9% of income in September 2026 versus 14.4% a year earlier ([Bank Indonesia](https://www.bi.go.id/id/publikasi/laporan/Documents/SK-September-2026.pdf), P1C2-18; U-D). Restaurant card spending grew 4.7% year-on-year in Q3 2026 (period inferred from the source paragraph), slower than 6.1% “last year”, yet still the fastest-growing monitored category ([Kontan relaying Bank Mandiri](https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/mandiri-spending-index-belanja-masyarakat-melambat-keuangan-kelas-bawah-tertekan), P1C2-29; N-D). A YouGov relay reports dining out among categories cut by 23% of Gen X+ respondents ([Marketing-Interactive relaying YouGov](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/study-68-of-indonesians-hopeful-amid-financial-strains-but-spending-splits-by-generation), P4W9-28; N-D), while 66% of urban Millennials named cooking at home as a money-saving strategy ([IDN Research Institute](https://cdn.gnfi.net/goodstats/uploads/publications/files/2025/08/27/indonesia-millennial-and-gen-z-report-2025-vvhOWWB6M6.pdf), P4W9-16; U-C). **Analysis:** a lower-priced coffee and drink ladder, smaller bundles and measurable repeat are risk controls, not optional merchandising.

**Foreign-brand performance and boycotts.** Analysis: foreign and foreign-branded operators show no single outcome. FAST/KFC reported 9M 2025 revenue down 0.76% and a net loss of IDR239.58bn, narrowed from IDR557.08bn in 9M 2024; its reporting also records 19 outlets closed and about 400 employees affected to September ([CNBC Indonesia](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/market/20251103181906-17-681892/tutup-20-gerai-pangkas-1041-karyawan-rugi-kfc--fast--turun-57), Q6-001/Q6-002; N-D), ([CNBC Indonesia](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/market/20251003175848-17-672723/masih-rugi--fast--tutup-19-gerai-phk-400-karyawan-tahun-ini), Q6-004; N-D). MAP Boga’s 9M 2025 sales fell 2.77% (computed from the filing) to IDR2.35tn and its net loss widened to IDR108.69bn (9M 2025 is the latest verified period); management said Starbucks had been removed from the boycott list and recovery was ongoing ([MAP Boga filing](https://www.mbai.co.id/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/9M-2025-MBA.pdf), Q6-005/Q6-006; N-D), ([CNBC Indonesia](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/market/20251105153124-17-682591/rugi-mapb-bengkak-jadi-rp108-m-starbucks-sudah-ditinggal-warga-ri), Q6-005/Q6-006; N-D). Conversely, Pizza Hut returned to IDR24.75bn profit in FY2025 from an IDR72.83bn loss, with sales around IDR3.05tn versus IDR2.79tn ([Detik Finance](https://finance.detik.com/bursa-dan-valas/d-8453630/beda-nasib-pizza-hut-kfc-satu-untung-satu-buntung), Q6-007/Q6-008; N-D). **Analysis:** these are operator outcomes, not proof that boycott caused or did not cause any one result; they show why CORSA should not extrapolate from a single brand.

Boycott sentiment is a reputational watchpoint: 80.2% agreed with boycotting Israel-affiliated products in a self-selected Indonesian online survey ([GoodStats relaying Median](https://goodstats.id/article/kenapa-publik-ri-setuju-untuk-boikot-produk-israel-n84Sj), S-04; Indonesia/2025/D), and 73.1% in a five-city survey (cities chosen for high boycott sensitivity, not Jakarta) agreed that some party benefits from the boycott, a different question ([RMOL relaying PKAEN](https://rmol.id/nusantara/read/2025/05/15/666584/), S-03; five Indonesian cities/2025/D). **Analysis:** neither figure estimates Jakarta prevalence or CORSA’s exposure. CORSA should make ownership, sourcing, halal status and local partnership legible, and monitor sentiment before launch.

## 10. Recommendations and strategic implications

**Keep — Analysis:** keep the compact flagship thesis, the coffee-plus-food third-place idea, community partnerships and an Indonesian operating network. The concept should retain Swiss/Italian distinctiveness, but the entry case must be a South/Central Jakarta test rather than a city-wide rollout.

**Adapt — Analysis:** adapt the economics around frequency: lead with coffee and functional drinks, offer a clear entry bundle, keep pasta quality and a small number of fast halal recipes, and make value visible in dine-in and delivery. Adapt the operating route toward an Indonesian partner or licensee while counsel checks franchise, STPW, PMA, tax and control boundaries. Treat local sourcing and halal documentation as customer-facing trust assets.

**Test — Analysis:** test three linked uncertainties before committing to a permanent fit-out: (1) paid covers and net ticket by daypart, (2) repeat purchase and bundle mix, and (3) site-level conversion in SCBD/Senopati against a lower-cost lifestyle corridor. The model’s base break-even is 136.71 guests/day and its base net spend is IDR103,375 (`Scenarios!D45` and `Scenarios!D8`; model v0.6). These are decision references, not observed demand. Evidence pointing the other way (Section 9): Indonesia’s middle class fell from 47.9m to 46.7m in 2024–25 (P1C2-01/P1C2-02; N-D); workers on middle-class wages fell from 14.5% to 7.1% in 2018–25 (P1C2-10; N-D); in the model the base case breaks even only at about 137 guests/day against 200 modelled, and the conservative case has negative EBITDA (Section 8; `Scenarios!D6`, `D45`, `Scenarios!C34`; model v0.6).

**Decision gates — Analysis:**

- **Go — Analysis:** proceed to a flagship only if a cleared operator/legal route and halal operating design exist, and a paid pilot reaches or beats the model base threshold (at least 137 guests/day, all channels at the base channel mix as defined in `Scenarios!D45`, at approximately IDR103,375 net spend) with positive contribution after verified staffing, rent, COGS and channel costs.
- **Adapt — Analysis:** continue testing if demand is between the optimistic and base break-even references (about 100–137 guests/day; `Scenarios!E45`/`D45`) or if drinks repeat but pasta does not. Change site, menu, bundle, hours or partner before signing a larger lease.
- **Stop — Analysis:** stop the Jakarta flagship path if two corridor/daypart tests remain below the optimistic break-even reference, repeat is not improving, or counsel cannot clear the operating structure and halal/import requirements within the approved capital frame. Preserve the brand option; do not fund a loss-making proof of optimism.

## 11. Ninety-day validation roadmap

**Days 1–30 — customer and proposition — Analysis:** recruit Jakarta residents and workers across 25–39 professionals, affluent social diners and run/padel communities; use intercepts near shortlisted sites, short online surveys and five-minute exit interviews. Ask about last three eating-out occasions, acceptable coffee/drink and pasta prices, frequency, dietary/halal requirements, delivery use, and what would make them return. Run menu tasting with two price ladders and record paid—not stated—choice, service time, waste and repeat intent.

**Days 31–60 — location and operating proof — Analysis:** count passers-by, seated guests, queue length, competitor covers and delivery pickups at SCBD, Senopati, Kuningan and one lower-cost lifestyle corridor across weekday lunch, weekday evening and weekend dayparts. The model’s district footfall fields are blank and its demand indices are judgement inputs (`Location!H8:N13`; model v0.6). Obtain at least two deal-rent quotes per finalist, verify usable ground-floor area, drainage/flood access, permits, service charges and halal kitchen constraints.

**Days 61–90 — paid pilot and decision — Analysis:** use a pop-up, partner counter or short event series rather than a long lease. Track daily paid covers, net ticket, drink/pasta mix, channel contribution, repeat within 30 days, acquisition source, refunds, prep time and waste. Reconcile observed results to the model and re-run the three gates in Section 10. **Assumption:** customer consented QRIS/loyalty identifiers can support repeat measurement; if not, use anonymous receipt cohorts and transparent opt-in.

**Primary-research brief — Analysis:** the customer module should interview customers, non-customers and venue operators; the location module should interview brokers, mall/corridor managers, delivery riders and comparable cafés; the legal module should interview an Indonesian counsel and halal adviser. Use structured scripts, quota the three priority occasions, preserve verbatim objections, and pre-register the decision rule before seeing results. No interview should be presented as market prevalence.
