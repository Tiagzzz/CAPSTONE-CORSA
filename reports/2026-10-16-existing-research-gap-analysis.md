## 1. Context digest

**CORSA and what it sells.** Zurich-born food and lifestyle brand: Italian racing heritage, healthy Mediterranean food (pasta), specialty coffee, functional drinks, community (SCOPE §1). Proof points are company-reported: Zurich HB pop-up, >200,000 people reached, 4.9-star Google rating, ROI after three months (SCOPE §1; KICK 37:20 "over 50,000 passers-by a day", from calculations on "SVB" data as transcribed; M3 38:57 about CHF 21k invested). The founder's July request also cites "high repeat rates" and six-digit social views (ANF), with no data attached.

**Project and scope history.** HSG Capstone for Prof. Bergmann (8 ECTS, 240 hours per student, groups of 3-6; PRX). Central question: how can CORSA enter Jakarta as first international flagship "in a commercially realistic, strategically coherent and culturally relevant way" (SCOPE §3). Phase 1 (to the interim presentation): Pillar 1 Strategic Fit incl. competitor and benchmark map, Pillar 2 financial model and business case (the core Phase 1 deliverable), joint 90-day roadmap, evidence appendix. Phase 2 (to 11 Dec): Pillar 3 Future Innovation, Food Tech and Physical AI (SCOPE §3, §8, §11). Earlier versions differ: the Aug blueprint has six workstreams incl. "franchise / investor logic" and a "Night Pit Stop" layer (BLU WS2, WS6), and states Jakarta is "not a Go / No-Go" and "the project should not aim to confirm assumptions" (BLU §1, §2); the 22 Sep scope still had the 90-day roadmap as Pillar 3 (SCV1 §8); the July request asked about franchise vs own operation (ANF).

**Team split.** Team A = Pillar 1: Ana (research and benchmarking), Matthieu (culture and geography), Tiago (experiential marketing and customer research) (M3 decisions 6, 7; M7 03:11).
