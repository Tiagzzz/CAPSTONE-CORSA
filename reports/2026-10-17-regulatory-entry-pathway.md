## 1. Summary (as of 2026-10-17; re-check BPJPH on 19-20 Oct 2026, because the halal phase for micro and small businesses starts on 18 Oct 2026)

**Deal-breakers for the current frame (one outlet, about CHF 200k):**

1. **Investment threshold.** A PMA must plan investment above IDR 10bn excluding land and building (the investment plan, not equity alone: its financing sources include loans, own capital, retained profit and share premium; Permen 5/2025 Art. 37(5), article number from the V-P2 verification of the opened text). For food and beverage services the base is the first 2 KBLI digits per "location point", meaning per kabupaten/kota, not per outlet. [E-04 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 26(2), 26(3)(b), 26(4)] PP 28/2025 repeats it. [E-05 · REG-002 · PP 28/2025 Art. 212(3)(b)] Missing the limit is a ground for administrative sanction. [E-04 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 364(3)(h)] No single-outlet, SME or phasing relief was found. Paid-up capital must be at least IDR 2.5bn per PT (about CHF 116k at the Plan 2 rate, not re-verified). [E-03 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 26(10)]
2. **Halal is mandatory for medium and large restaurants.** The phase ended on 17 Oct 2024 [H-02 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 160(1)]; "obligation from 18 Oct 2024" is BPJPH's reading of that article, not its wording. [H-02 · REG-010 · BPJPH statements of Oct 2024] A PMA is a large business in the investment rules. [E-07 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 26(1)] Analysis: if a PMA also counts as large for halal purposes (stated in no text; PP 42/2024 Art. 98(2) defers the scale test to other laws; question 12), the mandatory route applies from the first day. [H-03 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 98(2)] Halal place and tools must be separated from non-halal ones, including serving. [H-06 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 6(4), 20, 21, 22(3)] No text on certifying a corner of a mixed kitchen was found.
3. **Franchise profit test.** A franchisor needs 3 consecutive years of the franchised business plus 2 audited profitable years. [F-04 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 4(5)] Micro and small franchisors are exempt from the audit of the two financial statements; the profit evidence is still required, and whether a Swiss start-up counts as small is a question. [F-04 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 4(6)] Analysis: a young concept may fail the 3-year part. Permendag 71/2019, still "Berlaku", says 5 years. [F-05 · REG-030 · Permendag 71/2019 Art. 2(3)] Kontan (30 Jun 2025, tier 3) reports that Permendag 26/2025 revoked Permendag 36/2007, 22/2016, 25/2020 and 4/2023; 71/2019 is not among them, so which test applies stays a lawyer question (question 6). [F-05 · REG-901 · Kontan, 30 Jun 2025]
4. **Watch item.** A new investment Perpres is in a one-year drafting programme from 22 Dec 2025; none was found. [E-02 · REG-007 · Keppres 38/2025 Diktum Kedua, via DDTC, tier 3]

**Not barriers:** restaurants are open to foreign investors, no cap found [E-01 · REG-003 · Perpres 10/2021 Art. 3(1)(d), 3(2)]; the permit route is NIB plus Sertifikat Standar [E-09 · REG-002 · PP 28/2025 Art. 131, 132] (risk level and lead time unconfirmed, section 7); PBJT is 10% [T-01 · REG-019 · Perda DKI 1/2024 Art. 53(1)]; CIT is 22% [T-08 · REG-025 · UU PPh Art. 17(1)(b), as summarised by DJP].

Analysis: the lowest legal hurdle on the text is a franchise or licence to an Indonesian-owned operator, because the PMA threshold is not expected to attach to it (condition: no foreign shareholder in the operator; question 3). This holds if the PP 35/2024 gate is passed or the licence falls outside "Waralaba". An own PT PMA outlet needs an investment plan above IDR 10bn, about CHF 463k (Plan 2 rate, not re-verified), against the CHF 200k frame; equity is at least IDR 2.5bn and the rest may be financed (Art. 37(5); question 4b).

### 2.2 What the instruments say

**Investment and capital**
- Working capital for one turnover (wages, rent, operating costs) is part of the plan OSS validates for a PMA. [E-06 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 37(3), 37(4)]
- Paid-up capital stays in the company account for at least 12 months, except for assets, construction or operations. [E-03 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 27(1)]
- KBLI 56101 is food provision in a fixed building; 56303 is rumah minum/kafe. [E-12 · REG-005 · Peraturan BPS 7/2025]
- That the 2-digit group covers both codes is my reading (Analysis; question 2).

**Licences**
- Medium-high risk needs NIB plus a verified Sertifikat Standar; an unverified one is only a basis for preparation. [E-09 · REG-002 · PP 28/2025 Art. 132(1)-(6)]
- Hukumonline (tier 3) puts KBLI 56101 at large scale (PT PMA) at medium-high risk; the annex was not opened. [E-10 · REG-008 · PP 28/2025 Lampiran I.L]
- SLHS, a tourism certificate and RPTKA are tier-3 leads. [E-11 · REG-008 · PP 28/2025 Art. 170(e)]
- In a mall whose owner holds KKPR, PL, PBG and SLF, the tenant may use them; OSS verifies within 5 working days. No total lead time or fee was found. [E-13 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 41, 42]

**Halal**
- Food, drink, processing and serving must be certified. [H-01 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 2(1), 155, 159(2)(a)]
- Self-declaration exists only for micro and small businesses with low-risk products. [H-03 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 98(1), 98(2)] The BPJPH free scheme SEHATI (code SEHATI25) ran "Sepanjang tahun 2025 selama kuota masih tersedia"; it is not assumed to be running now, and a 2026 or 2027 round was not checked. [H-03 · REG-015 · BPJPH FAQ, undated]
- Products from forbidden ingredients are exempt but carry a visible non-halal statement. [H-05 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 2(2), 2(3), 110(1)]
- The BPJPH guideline covers restaurants, including franchise restaurants and branches: separation in every kitchen and outlet, a yearly internal audit, a 6-monthly report. [H-07 · REG-018 · Kepkaban 78/2023 Bab II A.2(a), B.3(b), B.5]
- BPJPH tariff per certificate: IDR 12,500,000 large and/or foreign-origin, IDR 5,000,000 medium, IDR 300,000 micro/small. [H-08 · REG-017 · Kepkaban 22/2024 Lampiran II A.2]
- LPH fee: unit cost ceiling row 17 (food and drink with processing) IDR 3,687,500 for medium, large and foreign, times 4 to 7 mandays by number of products; row 47 IDR 5,275,000 times 2 to 5 mandays. This is a unit cost, not a total. Plus an operational ceiling (large IDR 1,440,000; foreign IDR 1,920,000) and transport and allowance (DKI IDR 170,000 and 210,000 per manday). The LPH picks the row. The total needs an LPH quote (GAP). [H-09 · REG-017 · Kepkaban 22/2024 Lampiran IV]
- Maxima in working days: payment 5, inspection 15 (+10), MUI decision 3, certificate 1. [H-10 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 79(4), 82, 86(3), 88(1)]
- For breach of Art. 2(1): written warning and withdrawal of the product from circulation; certificate revocation and the IDR 2bn fine ceiling do not attach to it. BPJPH says closure applies to medium and large restaurants. [H-11 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 170(2), 170(7), 171(3)]
- BPJPH: no postponement of 18 Oct 2026; special provisions for UMK and imports in drafting (a press statement, not a regulation). [H-04 · REG-012 · BPJPH statement of 9 Oct 2026]
- Imports: the Minister fixes the duty by 17 Oct 2026; no decision was found. [H-12 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 160(3)] Foreign certificate registration costs IDR 800,000. [H-12 · REG-015 · BPJPH FAQ, Kepkaban 22/2024 Lampiran II A.3]

**Tax**
- PBJT on food and drink is 10%. [T-01 · REG-019 · Perda DKI 1/2024 Art. 53(1)] A restaurant has at least tables, chairs and/or utensils. [T-02 · REG-019 · Perda DKI 1/2024 Art. 45(1)(a)] The base is the payment received. [T-03 · REG-019 · Perda DKI 1/2024 Art. 51(1)(a)]
- Sales are excluded when monthly turnover is at most IDR 42,000,000. [T-04 · REG-019 · Perda DKI 1/2024 Art. 45(2)(a)]
- A 20% reduction applied to Aug-Dec 2025 and expired on 31 Jan 2026. [T-06 · REG-021 · Kepgub 722/2025 Diktum KESATU(b), KELIMA] March 2026 is per DDTC only. [T-07 · REG-022 · Kepgub 310/2026, relay]
- CIT is 22%; the DJP deck gives 11% only for turnover up to IDR 4.8bn. [T-08 · REG-025 · UU PPh Art. 17(1)(b), Art. 31E, as summarised by DJP; law text not opened]
- Withholding on a foreign taxpayer is 20% without treaty relief. [T-09 · REG-026 · UU PPh Art. 26(1)]
- Switzerland treaty ceilings: dividends 10% (company with at least 25% direct) or 15% [T-10 · REG-024 · Treaty Art. 10(2)]; royalties 10% [T-11 · REG-041 · 2007 Protocol Art. 2]; service fees 5% [T-13 · REG-024 · Treaty Art. 13(2)]. The 1988 text alone says 12.5% for royalties; the later instrument prevails. [T-12 · REG-024 · Treaty Art. 12(2)]
- VAT: 12% statutory, effective 11% via DPP 11/12 (relay; PMK not opened). [T-15 · REG-028 · PMK 131/2024 Pasal 3, via DDTC]

**Franchise**
- Every party needs an STPW through OSS. [F-02 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 12, 13(1), 14(1), 15(1)]
- A foreign franchisor adds a legalised home licence and a continuity statement from the Indonesian trade attache; the franchisee of a foreign franchise gets its STPW centrally. [F-03 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 13(3), 14(3), 15(2)]
- Regional STPW takes at most 5 working days and does not change the profit test; the 5 days do not cover the central STPW of a franchisee of a foreign franchise, whose lead time is unknown (question 9). [F-09 · REG-031 · Permendag 25/2025 Art. 4(11), 4(12)]
- Prospectus at least 2 weeks before signing; agreement in Bahasa Indonesia. [F-08 · REG-030 · Permendag 71/2019 Art. 5, 6, 7]
- Domestic goods are to be prioritised, for franchisees only as far as they meet the franchisor's written quality standards (Art. 26(2), (4)); no quota is stated. [F-06 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 26, 27] Art. 26(1), (3), (5) bind domestic franchisors only (Art. 3(b)-(d), per the V-P2 verification).
- Foreign franchisors and franchisees of foreign franchises are regulated parties. [F-07 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 1(1), 3(a), 3(e)]

### 2.3 Route (a): PT PMA, own outlet

Text: a PT under Indonesian law is required. [E-07 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 23(11)] The investment plan, paid-up capital, NIB, Sertifikat Standar, halal duty and tax rows above all apply to the PT.

Analysis: most control, highest capital need. The 2-digit rule means a second outlet in the same city adds no second IDR 10bn base, but the first outlet must clear it.

### 2.4 Route (b): franchise or licence to a local partner

Text: the franchisor and the local operator need an STPW and the franchisor must pass the profit test (above). Fees paid to a Swiss licensor may be royalties (10%) or service fees (5%) at the treaty ceilings above (question 17). [T-11 · REG-041 · 2007 Protocol Art. 2] [T-13 · REG-024 · Treaty Art. 13(2)] The franchise is defined as a right to use an intellectual-property or business system. [F-07 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 1(1)]

Analysis: the operator would hold the NIB, the halal certificate and the PBJT duty (inference, not tagged). A franchise to a local licensee is not expected to trigger the PT PMA investment threshold (this rests on a worker note in F-07, not on Art. 1(1), which is only the definition). That holds only if the operator is Indonesian-owned; the foreign-share cut-off for PMA status is not stated in the texts read (question 3). Whether a pure trademark and know-how licence falls outside "Waralaba" is open (question 8). The halal and quality burden sits with a third party but remains a brand risk.

### 2.5 Route (c): joint venture

Text: a legal entity "may be classed" PMDN or PMA, and the PMA definition is deferred to investment-law rules. [E-07 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 23(9), 1(21)] No relief was found for a PMA with a local partner. [E-04 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 26] Paid-up capital is IDR 2.5bn per PT. [E-03 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 26(10)]

Analysis: unless a lawyer confirms that a minority foreign stake keeps the PT outside PMA, a JV meets the threshold like route (a); it shares the capital, not the rule. Dividends to a Swiss holder below 25% direct would bear the 15% rate. [T-10 · REG-024 · Treaty Art. 10(2)(b)]

## 3. What this means for the single-outlet assumption

Analysis: the CHF 200k frame does not carry a PT PMA. The plan converts IDR 10bn to about CHF 463k (Plan 2 figure, not re-verified), about 2.3 times the frame. Equipment, other investment including pre-opening operating costs during construction, and one turnover of working capital count toward the plan; only land and building are excluded. [E-06 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 37(4)(a)-(e), 26(2)] The plan may be financed with own capital, loans, retained profit and share premium (Art. 37(5)); whether shareholder loans qualify is open (question 4b). Whether leasehold fit-out is "bangunan" and whether deposits count is open (question 4).

Three options, none legally cleared: (1) raise capital above the threshold and open one flagship; (2) **license or franchise to an Indonesian operator, the route to test first**, accepting the PP 35/2024 gate and royalty income instead of outlet profit; (3) a JV that funds the threshold jointly. Halal certificate costs are small next to the threshold, but separation drives kitchen design and menu: a mixed kitchen is not supported by the text. Analysis: the Perpres rewrite is an upside option only; nothing suggests it lowers the Permen 5/2025 threshold.
