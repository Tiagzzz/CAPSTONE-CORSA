# Regulatory and entry-route pathway: CORSA in Jakarta (Plan 2)

Date: 2026-10-17. Author: P2-A9. Status: draft for owner review. Research, not legal advice (section 8).

**Reading guide.** Each legal statement ends with a tag `[row · register_id · instrument and article]`. Rows are in the Plan 2 CSVs, `listed-operators.csv` or `plan4-origin-boycott.csv`, all VERIFIED-OPENED or CORRECTED-OPENED. Tagged sentences report what the instrument says. "Analysis:" marks my interpretation, which has no authority. In-force status means "Berlaku" on the JDIH pages opened on 2026-10-17.

## 1. Summary

**Deal-breakers for the current frame (one outlet, about CHF 200k):**

1. **Investment threshold.** A PMA must plan investment above IDR 10bn excluding land and building. For food and beverage services the base is the first 2 KBLI digits per "location point", meaning per kabupaten/kota, not per outlet. [E-04 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 26(2), 26(3)(b), 26(4)] PP 28/2025 repeats it. [E-05 · REG-002 · PP 28/2025 Art. 212(3)(b)] Missing the limit is a ground for administrative sanction. [E-04 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 364(3)(h)] No single-outlet, SME or phasing relief was found. Paid-up capital must be at least IDR 2.5bn per PT. [E-03 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 26(10)]
2. **Halal is mandatory for medium and large restaurants.** The phase ended on 17 Oct 2024; the obligation runs from 18 Oct 2024. [H-02 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 160(1)] A PMA is a large business in the investment rules. [E-07 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 26(1)] Halal place and tools must be separated from non-halal ones, including serving. [H-06 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 6(4), 20, 21, 22(3)] No text on certifying a corner of a mixed kitchen was found.
3. **Franchise profit test.** A franchisor needs 3 consecutive years of the franchised business plus 2 audited profitable years. [F-04 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 4(5)] A young concept may fail. Permendag 71/2019, still "Berlaku", says 5 years. [F-05 · REG-030 · Permendag 71/2019 Art. 2(3)]
4. **Watch item.** A new investment Perpres is in a one-year drafting programme from 22 Dec 2025; none was found. [E-02 · REG-007 · Keppres 38/2025 Diktum Kedua, via DDTC, tier 3]

**Not barriers:** restaurants are open to foreign investors, no cap found [E-01 · REG-003 · Perpres 10/2021 Art. 3(1)(d), 3(2)]; the permit is NIB plus Sertifikat Standar [E-09 · REG-002 · PP 28/2025 Art. 131, 132]; PBJT is 10% [T-01 · REG-019 · Perda DKI 1/2024 Art. 53(1)]; CIT is 22% [T-08 · REG-025 · UU PPh Art. 17(1)(b), as summarised by DJP].

Analysis: the lowest legal hurdle on the text is a franchise or licence to an Indonesian-owned operator, because the PMA threshold does not attach to it. It depends on passing the PP 35/2024 profit test or on the licence falling outside "Waralaba". An own PT PMA outlet needs capital far above CHF 200k.

### 2.2 What the instruments say

**Investment and capital**
- Working capital for one turnover (wages, rent, operating costs) is part of the plan OSS validates for a PMA. [E-06 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 37(3), 37(4)]
- Paid-up capital stays in the company account for at least 12 months, except for assets, construction or operations. [E-03 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 27(1)]
- KBLI 56101 is food provision in a fixed building; 56303 is rumah minum/kafe. [E-12 · REG-005 · Peraturan BPS 7/2025]
- That the 2-digit group covers both codes is my reading (Analysis; question 2).

**Licences**
- Medium-high risk needs NIB plus a verified Sertifikat Standar; an unverified one is only a basis for preparation. [E-09 · REG-002 · PP 28/2025 Art. 132(1)-(6)]
- Hukumonline (tier 3) puts KBLI 56101 at large scale (PT PMA) at medium-high risk; the annex was not opened. [E-10 · REG-008 · PP 28/2025 Lampiran I.L]
- SLHS, a tourism certificate and RPTKA are tier-3 leads. [E-11 · REG-008 · PP 28/2025 Art. 170(e)]
- In a mall whose owner holds KKPR, PL, PBG and SLF, the tenant may use them; OSS verifies within 5 working days. No total lead time or fee was found. [E-13 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 41, 42]

**Halal**
- Food, drink, processing and serving must be certified. [H-01 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 2(1), 155, 159(2)(a)]
- Self-declaration exists only for micro and small businesses with low-risk products. [H-03 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 98(1), 98(2)]
- Products from forbidden ingredients are exempt but carry a visible non-halal statement. [H-05 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 2(2), 2(3), 110(1)]
- The BPJPH guideline covers restaurants, including franchise restaurants and branches: separation in every kitchen and outlet, a yearly internal audit, a 6-monthly report. [H-07 · REG-018 · Kepkaban 78/2023 Bab II A.2(a), B.3(b), B.5]
- BPJPH tariff per certificate: IDR 12,500,000 large and/or foreign-origin, IDR 5,000,000 medium, IDR 300,000 micro/small. [H-08 · REG-017 · Kepkaban 22/2024 Lampiran II A.2]
- LPH fee ceiling: row 17 (food and drink with processing) IDR 3,687,500 for medium, large and foreign; row 47 IDR 5,275,000. The LPH picks the row. [H-09 · REG-017 · Kepkaban 22/2024 Lampiran IV]
- Maxima in working days: payment 5, inspection 15 (+10), MUI decision 3, certificate 1. [H-10 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 79(4), 82, 86(3), 88(1)]
- For breach of Art. 2(1): warning and withdrawal; the IDR 2bn fine ceiling does not attach to it. BPJPH says closure applies to medium and large restaurants. [H-11 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 170(2), 170(7), 171(3)]
- BPJPH: no postponement of 18 Oct 2026; special provisions for UMK and imports in drafting (a press statement, not a regulation). [H-04 · REG-012 · BPJPH statement of 9 Oct 2026]
- Imports: the Minister fixes the duty by 17 Oct 2026; no decision was found. [H-12 · REG-009 · PP 42/2024 Art. 160(3)] Foreign certificate registration costs IDR 800,000. [H-12 · REG-015 · BPJPH FAQ, Kepkaban 22/2024 Lampiran II A.3]

**Tax**
- PBJT on food and drink is 10%. [T-01 · REG-019 · Perda DKI 1/2024 Art. 53(1)] A restaurant has at least tables, chairs and/or utensils. [T-02 · REG-019 · Perda DKI 1/2024 Art. 45(1)(a)] The base is the payment received. [T-03 · REG-019 · Perda DKI 1/2024 Art. 51(1)(a)]
- Sales are excluded when monthly turnover is at most IDR 42,000,000. [T-04 · REG-019 · Perda DKI 1/2024 Art. 45(2)(a)]
- A 20% reduction applied to Aug-Dec 2025 and expired on 31 Jan 2026. [T-06 · REG-021 · Kepgub 722/2025 Diktum KESATU(b), KELIMA] March 2026 is per DDTC only. [T-07 · REG-022 · Kepgub 310/2026, relay]
- CIT is 22%; the DJP deck gives 11% only for turnover up to IDR 4.8bn. [T-08 · REG-025 · UU PPh Art. 17(1)(b), Art. 31E]
- Withholding on a foreign taxpayer is 20% without treaty relief. [T-09 · REG-026 · UU PPh Art. 26(1)]
- Switzerland treaty ceilings: dividends 10% (company with at least 25% direct) or 15% [T-10 · REG-024 · Treaty Art. 10(2)]; royalties 10% [T-11 · REG-041 · 2007 Protocol Art. 2]; service fees 5% [T-13 · REG-024 · Treaty Art. 13(2)]. The 1988 text alone says 12.5% for royalties; the later instrument prevails. [T-12 · REG-024 · Treaty Art. 12(2)]
- VAT: 12% statutory, effective 11% via DPP 11/12 (relay; PMK not opened). [T-15 · REG-028 · PMK 131/2024 Pasal 3, via DDTC]

**Franchise**
- Every party needs an STPW through OSS. [F-02 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 12, 13(1), 14(1), 15(1)]
- A foreign franchisor adds a legalised home licence and a continuity statement from the Indonesian trade attache; the franchisee of a foreign franchise gets its STPW centrally. [F-03 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 13(3), 14(3), 15(2)]
- Regional STPW takes at most 5 working days and does not change the profit test. [F-09 · REG-031 · Permendag 25/2025 Art. 4(11), 4(12)]
- Prospectus at least 2 weeks before signing; agreement in Bahasa Indonesia. [F-08 · REG-030 · Permendag 71/2019 Art. 5, 6, 7]
- Domestic goods are to be prioritised; no quota is stated. [F-06 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 26, 27]
- Foreign franchisors and franchisees of foreign franchises are regulated parties. [F-07 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 1(1), 3(a), 3(e)]

### 2.3 Route (a): PT PMA, own outlet

Text: a PT under Indonesian law is required. [E-07 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 23(11)] The investment plan, paid-up capital, NIB, Sertifikat Standar, halal duty and tax rows above all apply to the PT.

Analysis: most control, highest capital need. The 2-digit rule means a second outlet in the same city adds no second IDR 10bn base, but the first outlet must clear it.

### 2.4 Route (b): franchise or licence to a local partner

Text: the franchisor and the local operator need an STPW and the franchisor must pass the profit test (above). The operator holds the NIB, the halal certificate and the PBJT duty. Fees paid to a Swiss licensor are royalties, taxed at the treaty ceiling above or the domestic rate. A franchise to a local licensee does not trigger the PT PMA investment threshold, per the data row. [F-07 · REG-029 · PP 35/2024 Art. 1(1)]

Analysis: that holds only if the operator is Indonesian-owned; the foreign-share cut-off for PMA status is not stated in the texts read (question 3). Whether a pure trademark and know-how licence falls outside "Waralaba" is open (question 8). The halal and quality burden sits with a third party but remains a brand risk.

### 2.5 Route (c): joint venture

Text: a legal entity "may be classed" PMDN or PMA, and the PMA definition is deferred to investment-law rules. [E-07 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 23(9), 1(21)] No relief was found for a PMA with a local partner. [E-04 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 26] Paid-up capital is IDR 2.5bn per PT. [E-03 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 26(10)]

Analysis: unless a lawyer confirms that a minority foreign stake keeps the PT outside PMA, a JV meets the threshold like route (a); it shares the capital, not the rule. Dividends to a Swiss holder below 25% direct would bear the 15% rate. [T-10 · REG-024 · Treaty Art. 10(2)(b)]

## 3. What this means for the single-outlet assumption

Analysis: the CHF 200k frame does not carry a PT PMA. The plan converts IDR 10bn to about CHF 463k (Plan 2 figure, not re-verified), about 2.3 times the frame. Working capital counts toward the plan [E-06 · REG-001 · Permen 5/2025 Art. 37(4)(e)]; whether lease fit-out, deposits and pre-opening costs count is open (question 4).

Three options, none legally cleared: (1) raise capital above the threshold and open one flagship; (2) **license or franchise to an Indonesian operator, the route to test first**, accepting the PP 35/2024 gate and royalty income instead of outlet profit; (3) a JV that funds the threshold jointly. Halal certificate costs are small next to the threshold, but separation drives kitchen design and menu: a mixed kitchen is not supported by the text. The Perpres rewrite is an upside option only; nothing suggests it lowers the Permen 5/2025 threshold.

Analysis: rates are stable on paper; budgets should not rely on discounts.

[S-09 · REG-043 · BKPM release of 15 Jan 2026] Jan-Jun 2026: IDR 1,010.6 trillion (+7.2%).
