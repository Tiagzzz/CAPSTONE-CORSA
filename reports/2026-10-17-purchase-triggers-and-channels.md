Date: 2026-10-17. Plan: `tasks/2026-10-16-plan-4-purchase-triggers-channels.md`. Status: DRAFT for verifiers V1-V4; not yet accepted by the Product Owner. Companion dataset: `data/processed/plan4-trigger-matrix.csv` (all rows and cells, including every GAP).

**How to read the citations.** Every figure carries a tag `[row id; register id; reference period; base; geography; age-fit]`. Row ids point to the CSV files in `data/processed/`; register ids to `sources/sources.md`. Geography: J = Jakarta, U = urban Indonesia (several cities, usually Java), N = national, P = proxy (other category or other countries). Age-fit: A exact, B or C partial overlap with the canonical bands, D = no age split or no usable overlap. Only rows whose source was opened (VERIFIED-OPENED or CORRECTED-OPENED) are used; BLOCKED rows appear only in the boxed "provisional" list. Text starting "Analysis:" is interpretation; everything else is sourced fact or a plain description of the dataset. Nothing is interpolated, summed across answer categories, or scaled to Jakarta.

## Summary

- **Evidence base.** No opened row gives a 25-39 cut. Jakarta-specific (J) rows are few; most are national online panels (N) or Java-urban samples that exclude Jakarta (U). Findings are directional, not Jakarta estimates.
- **Verdicts.** H1 not supported as stated; H2 insufficient evidence; H3 supported at the stated, price-capped level; H4 insufficient evidence; H5 insufficient evidence; H6 insufficient evidence; H7 insufficient evidence; H8 insufficient evidence.
- **Discovery.** In the only direct channel comparison, friends and family come first: 44% [P4W1-R04; BEH-004; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D] against TikTok food influencer accounts 40% [P4W1-R05; BEH-004; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D]. Multiple answers, margin of error under five points, no Jakarta, no age split: the routes are close, and social is not shown to lead.
- **First visit and return.** Price and taste lead: affordable price attracts 64% [UA-N58; BEH-040; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D]; tasty menu is the top revisit reason, 83, and aesthetics the last, 27 [P4W7b-14; BEH-066; 14-19 Oct 2025; n=932; N; D]
- **Health** is a real but price-capped motive: more important than five years ago 65% [P4W4-04; BEH-013; 2025 survey; n not stated; N; D]; willing to pay more than 10% extra 48% [P4W4-01; BEH-013; 2025 survey; n not stated; N; D]; 20% extra 19% [P4W4-02; BEH-013; 2025 survey; n not stated; N; D].
- **Identity transfer.** Racing, Italian origin and functional drinks have no audience or association data in the opened rows. The only racing figure is Mandalika attendance 140,324 (spectators) [Q8-001; BEH-027; 3-5 Oct 2025; n not stated; N; D] with no demographics. They stay hypotheses.
- **Biggest gaps.** Any 25-39 or Jakarta cut; repeat visits; what raises basket size; whether run or padel clubs convert into F&B purchases; how a Swiss-Italian brand is read in Indonesia.

## Findings by trigger

- Reasons to try a new place (same survey, other item): social media or ads 44% [P4W2b-03; BEH-071; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=769; U; D]; good reviews or ratings 42% [P4W2b-02; BEH-071; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=769; U; D]. The row records an unresolved base conflict between two article counts.
- Trust in decision sources (score out of four, six SEA markets, not food-specific): family and friends 2.42, online reviews 2.36, creators 1.98 [P4W2b-11; BEH-006; impact.com x Cube x Dentsu 2026 edition; n=2400; P; D]

- Qualitative: micro and nano influencers hold trust better than larger tiers [P4W2b-06; BEH-005; impact.com x Cube 2025 edition; n=2400; P; D]; trust in food-vlogger reviews fell after a transparency scandal [P4W2b-21; BEH-079; 2025-06-16; n=85; N; C]; Google Maps ratings raised customer trust in two Surakarta restaurants [P4W2b-19; BEH-077; 2026-01-30; n=400; U; D]; TikTok and Instagram content raised Gen Z purchase intention in Situbondo, TikTok more [P4W2b-20; BEH-078; 2026-04-26; n=100; U; C]

Analysis: Friends, TikTok food accounts and social ads sit close together as discovery routes, none with an age cut. The rows neither show that social platforms dominate nor that they do not matter. The creator-link headline overstates conversion once opening and buying are separated.

Analysis, ranked by strength of evidence (all stated, none age-specific):

1. Price and promotion drive trial and app choice; they also compress the basket, so they are a conversion lever with a margin cost.
2. Taste and menu drive trial and return; a tasty menu is the leading revisit reason.
3. Peer recommendation and TikTok food accounts jointly drive discovery; neither is shown to lead by more than the margin of error.
4. Reviews and ratings are both a trigger and a brake.
5. Comfort and a place to gather support return visits but rank below taste.

Analysis: health is a modest basket lever at most. Payment method, live commerce and creator links are not shown to raise spend.

## Hypotheses H1-H8

Each verdict is tested against the plan's own disconfirming criterion (plan 4, section 2). Scale: supported / not supported / insufficient evidence.

### H1 Social discovery is the main first-visit trigger for 25-39 urban consumers

- Evidence for: TikTok food influencer accounts 40% [P4W1-R05; BEH-004; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D] and social ads 39% [P4W1-R06; BEH-004; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D] are close behind the leading route; social media or ads is a stated reason to try 44% [P4W2b-03; BEH-071; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=769; U; D].
- Evidence against: friends and family come first 44% [P4W1-R04; BEH-004; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D]; trust ranks family and friends (2.42) above creators (1.98) [P4W2b-11; BEH-006; impact.com x Cube x Dentsu 2026 edition; n=2400; P; D] trust in food-vlogger reviews fell [P4W2b-21; BEH-079; 2025-06-16; n=85; N; C]
- Test: confirmation needs social ranked above friends, Maps and offline in at least two independent sources in the 25-39 bands. There is one direct comparison, no 25-39 cut and no Jakarta cut. The disconfirmer "friends or word of mouth lead" is met nominally, within the margin of error.
- **Verdict: not supported as stated** (not shown to be the main trigger; one of several close routes).
- Entry change: move part of the creator-seeding budget to peer referral (bring-a-friend mechanics), Maps and review presence and delivery-app promotions; test creators as one channel, not the lead.

### H2 Novelty and viral appeal trigger trial, especially for Gen Z and younger Millennials

- Evidence for: would try a new place 73% [P4W2b-04; BEH-071; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=769; U; D]; unique menu attracts 49% [UA-N59; BEH-040; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D].
- Evidence against: early adopters are few 7% [P4W3-04; BEH-010; September 2024; n=2136; N; D] and need-based shoppers many 52% [P4W3-08; BEH-010; September 2024; n=2136; N; D]; many wait for reviews 40% [UA-N61; BEH-040; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D]; Millennials, not Gen Z, are described as more open to trends [UB-02; BEH-009; Feb 2025 fieldwork; n=1100; N; D]; aesthetics rank last among revisit reasons, 27 [P4W7b-14; BEH-066; 14-19 Oct 2025; n=932; N; D]
- Test: the disconfirmers are small effects with weak repeat, low scores for older bands, and short-lived viral venues. Repeat and venue life are not measured; the age pattern runs against "especially Gen Z", but only in one qualitative D-coded row.
- **Verdict: insufficient evidence.**
- Entry change: do not rely on launch buzz; build the opening around taste and a repeatable routine (lunch, coffee) and measure repeat in the first weeks. If repeat is low, adopt the plan's fallback of positioning on routine.

### H3 Health motivation is real but changes purchases only modestly and mostly at constant price

- Evidence for: intent is high 65% [P4W4-04; BEH-013; 2025 survey; n not stated; N; D]; willingness to pay falls from the lower to the higher premium, 48% [P4W4-01; BEH-013; 2025 survey; n not stated; N; D] then 19% [P4W4-02; BEH-013; 2025 survey; n not stated; N; D]; cost is a barrier 43% [P4W4-06; BEH-013; 2025 survey; n not stated; N; D].
- Evidence against: no opened row shows health as a top-three choice driver with observed uplift in Jakarta; the choice-driver lists printed are price 60% [UA-N65; BEH-040; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D] and taste 59% [P4W7b-02; BEH-040; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D]. That is absence of a row, not proof that health has no effect.
- Test: confirmation (high stated intent plus price-capped behaviour) is met at the stated level; the disconfirmer (top-three driver with observed uplift in Jakarta) is not met, but observed behaviour is untested.
- **Verdict: supported** (stated and price-capped; low confidence; national, no age split, food and wellness products).
- Entry change: lead with taste, convenience and "balanced"; health is a secondary claim; keep any health line within a modest premium.

### H4 Run clubs and padel communities are a viable acquisition channel for the 25-39 core

- Evidence for: participation is large and growing: festival 27,300 (runners) [P4W5-04; BEH-017; Oct 2025; n=27300; J; D], growth 70% [UC-N04; BEH-017; Oct 2025 vs Oct 2024; n not stated; J; D], Strava clubs about 6x ("hampir 6x lipat") [UC-N02; BEH-016; Mid-Year Year in Sport 2025 vs 2024; n not stated; N; D]; Jakarta padel clubs are described as underserved +50 clubs for 25M inhabitants [UC-N06; BEH-042; 2024/25; n not stated; J; D].
- Evidence against or unknown: no verified row on age mix, cafe use, sponsorship or purchase influence; Strava is self-selected; padel permit problems 185 of 397 (about 46) [UC-N07; BEH-044; Mar 2026; n=397; J; D].
- Test: the disconfirmers are a small elite or other age bands, no purchase-influence evidence, a gear-led community. Purchase-influence evidence is missing: a gap, not a negative result.
- **Verdict: insufficient evidence.**
- Entry change: treat community as a brand-building test (two tracked events, organiser interviews); keep delivery and office-lunch channels as the base case until club-level conversion is measured.

### H5 Italian or European origin is a mild positive or neutral signal

- Evidence for: boycott effects are visible at specific US-linked chains, with a profit recovery at one 24.75 profit vs 72.83 loss [Q6-007; BEH-052; FY2025 vs FY2024; n not stated; N; D] and management reporting improving sentiment [UC-N11; BEH-048; Nov 2025; n not stated; N; D]; consistent with brand-specific boycotts, but not a test.
- Evidence against: stated preference for local culinary businesses 77% [UA-N66; BEH-040; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D]; continuing net losses at two chains 239.58 vs 557.08 [Q6-001; BEH-045; Jan-Sep 2025 vs Jan-Sep 2024; n not stated; N; D] and 108.69 vs 79.13 [Q6-005; BEH-048; Jan-Sep 2025 vs Jan-Sep 2024; n not stated; N; D].
- Test: country-of-origin evidence for Gen Z, a quality or innovation link and Italian-specific associations are absent. The disconfirmers (significant ethnocentrism, a boycott that generalises to foreign F&B, Italian read as expensive and not daily) are neither shown nor ruled out.
- **Verdict: insufficient evidence.**
- Entry change: lead with product and local partnerships, not "foreign import"; pre-empt political framing; test spontaneous associations and perceived foreign ownership.

### H6 Racing identity resonates with the target

- Evidence for: Mandalika MotoGP attendance 140,324 (spectators) [Q8-001; BEH-027; 3-5 Oct 2025; n not stated; N; D]; growth versus 2024 15.7% [Q8-004; BEH-029; 2025 vs 2024; n not stated; N; D].
- Evidence against or unknown: counts carry no age, sex, income or city; the circuit is outside Jakarta; no overlap with cafe or run segments is measured.
- Test: confirmation needs a young, urban, sizeable Jakarta audience overlapping the target; none is evidenced. The disconfirmers (male or regional, no overlap, "fast food" reading) are untested too.
- **Verdict: insufficient evidence.**
- Entry change: racing as a secondary design and story layer, not the lead message, until a concept-card test reads it.

### H7 Functional drinks, community and third place transfer

- Evidence for: third-place use is documented: gathering 44% [UC-N13; BEH-030; mid-2025; n=1002; N; D], working 34% [P4W7b-01; BEH-030; mid-2025; n=1002; N; D], self-reward 49.1% [P4W7b-13; BEH-066; 14-19 Oct 2025; n=932; N; D]; trend-drink consumption exists, matcha among Gen Z 34% [P4W9-03a; BEH-082; 22-24 Apr 2026; n=1238; N; D].
- Evidence against or unknown: aesthetics rank last among revisit reasons, 27 [P4W7b-14; BEH-066; 14-19 Oct 2025; n=932; N; D] the Jakarta Gen Z servicescape result was not significant [P4W7b-15; BEH-067; Peer-reviewed article, Vol. 19 No. 1, February 2; n=500; J; B] label scepticism: clearer labels wanted 62% [UB-14; BEH-013; Jan-Feb 2025; n not stated; N; D], doubts that health products work 43% [P4W4-07; BEH-013; 2025 survey; n not stated; N; D]. No series shows functional-drink uptake rising.
- Test: the third-place half is supported at the national, stated level; the functional-drink half has no growth evidence and some label scepticism.
- **Verdict: insufficient evidence** (third place supported, functional drinks insufficient).
- Entry change: drinks in a supporting role; test the menu before any drinks-led concept; build the venue around taste, service and comfort.

### H8 Halal cues influence venue choice for the Muslim-majority market

- Evidence for: consumers buying depending on a halal label 83% [P4W7b-18; BEH-069; Cited on 2025-06-21 from a Populix report fielde; n not stated; N; D] (relay of a vendor survey, all products, before the window).

## Disconfirming evidence for CORSA's entry assumptions

Facts that challenge the assumptions (each is a reason to test before spending):

1. **Social is not clearly first.** Friends and family 44% [P4W1-R04; BEH-004; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D] against TikTok food accounts 40% [P4W1-R05; BEH-004; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D].
2. **Price first.** Price decides where to eat 60% [UA-N65; BEH-040; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D]; Gen Z coffee consumers name price the main factor 47% [UE-N03; DEM-001; 6-9 Dec 2024; n=896; N; B]. A premium-but-accessible position must clear this.
3. **Frugality.** Frugal lifestyle, Gen Z 56% [P4W9-19; BEH-086; Mar-Aug 2024; n=750; U; C] and Millennials 49% [P4W9-18; BEH-086; Mar-Aug 2024; n=750; U; C]; Millennials cook at home 66% [P4W9-16; BEH-086; Mar-Aug 2024; n=750; U; C]. Restaurant spending growth: restaurants +4.7% yoy in Q3 2026 vs +6.1% in 2025; overall MSI growth 5.9% Q3 2026 (Sep 5.5%, lowest since Nov 2025) [P1C2-29; DEM-046; Q3 2026; n not stated; N; D]
4. **Thin novelty base.** Early adopters 7% [P4W3-04; BEH-010; September 2024; n=2136; N; D]; waiting for reviews 40% [UA-N61; BEH-040; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D] against the grand opening 16% [UA-N64; BEH-040; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D].
5. **Atmosphere is not the return reason.** Aesthetics 27, last in the list [P4W7b-14; BEH-066; 14-19 Oct 2025; n=932; N; D]; the Jakarta Gen Z servicescape effect was not significant [P4W7b-15; BEH-067; Peer-reviewed article, Vol. 19 No. 1, February 2; n=500; J; B]
6. **Creator and live conversion is weaker than headlines.** Opened 83 versus purchased under 40 [P4W2b-14; BEH-074; Jakpat e-commerce trends 2H 2025 follow-up; n=1714; N; D] live purchase rate series 46, 44 [P4W2b-16; BEH-075; Jakpat semi-annual surveys; n not stated; N; D]
7. **Local preference and boycott spillover.** 77% [UA-N66; BEH-040; 2-7 Sep 2025; n=995; U; D]; the KFC operator's continuing loss 239.58 vs 557.08 [Q6-001; BEH-045; Jan-Sep 2025 vs Jan-Sep 2024; n not stated; N; D].
8. **Community channel risk.** Jakarta padel courts without a permit: 185 of 397 (about 46) [UC-N07; BEH-044; Mar 2026; n=397; J; D]
9. **Health premium ceiling.** 19% [P4W4-02; BEH-013; 2025 survey; n not stated; N; D]

Facts pointing the other way, for balance: Jakarta consumer confidence 146.8 (Sep 2026) [P1C2-20; DEM-034; September 2026; n not stated; J; D]; spending growth in all generations in the Ramadan to Lebaran window (seasonal) Gen Z +4.4%, Millennials +3%, Gen X +1.4% [P1C2-32; DEM-048; Ramadan to Lebaran 2026; n not stated; N; C]; the Pizza Hut operator's recovery 24.75 profit vs 72.83 loss [Q6-007; BEH-052; FY2025 vs FY2024; n not stated; N; D].

Analysis: the assumptions most at risk are "social media first", "premium without a price barrier" and "atmosphere and novelty bring people back". The evidence is stated and mostly national, so these are reasons to test, not proofs of failure. No collectivism or national-culture argument is used anywhere in this report; group effects appear only where a survey item measures them (friends and family invitations to mall visits 65% [UE-N06; DEM-002; Jan-Apr 2025; n=266; J; D]).

## Method and limits

- **Inputs and rules.** Rows with verification VERIFIED-OPENED or CORRECTED-OPENED in `data/processed/plan4-*.csv` and, for spend context only, `data/processed/demand/*.csv`. No new research and no new figures: each figure is copied from a row and carries that row's register id, period, base and geography.
- **Tiers.** APJII (BEH-037, BEH-038), Playtomic (BEH-042) and the journal articles (for example BEH-011, BEH-067) are tier 2; Bank Indonesia releases (BEH-062, BEH-063) and the MAPB filing (DEM-025) are tier 1; every other source (Jakpat, Populix, YouGov, impact.com, Strava, news relays, data portals) is tier 3. Tier 3 online-panel surveys reach online respondents only and serve as directional evidence. No claim rests on tier 4 alone.
- **Relay versus original.** Several rows were verified on a relay (GoodStats, Databoks, trade press) without opening the original; the register says which. Single-relay figures are weaker. Where measures conflict, this report puts them side by side (for example the creator-link headline versus purchase).
- **Geography.** Most rows are national or Java-urban; the Jakpat September 2025 survey excludes Jakarta. Jakarta-coded rows cover the running festival, padel permits, a Gen Z study, mall visitors (Jabodetabek malls) and per-capita spend. Nothing is scaled from national to Jakarta.
- **Age-fit.** D-coded rows are shown and flagged in the matrix, never aggregated or interpolated. The Millennial band is not split into Younger and Older. The 25-39 column is a documented gap.
- **Time.** Some rows predate the preferred window (halal survey fieldwork 2023; IMGR fieldwork 2024) and are flagged in their notes.
- **Bias guards.** No national-culture index is converted into behaviour; no collectivism-as-opportunity claim is made; "no data" is reported as a gap and never as "no effect"; every hypothesis lists evidence against.
- **Status.** Awaiting verifiers V1-V4 (figures against sources, register and tiers, links, bias read-through).
