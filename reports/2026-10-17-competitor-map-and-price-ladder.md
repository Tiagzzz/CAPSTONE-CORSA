Date: 2026-10-17. Author: P3-E-synth (synthesis writer, no new research). Status: draft for verification. Input: only rows coded VERIFIED-OPENED or CORRECTED-OPENED in `data/processed/listed-operators.csv` (rows P3BF-), `data/processed/foreign-entrants.csv` (rows P3B-) and `data/processed/price-ladder.csv` (rows P3PR-, P3PB-), plus the DERIVED table in `data/raw/2026-10-17-price-points.md`. **Conventions.** Each figure carries (register_id; period; geography). ## 1. Summary

- **Listed operators.** The mass and mid-market chains are under pressure but not uniformly. Fast Food Indonesia (FAST, KFC) lost Rp61.1 bn in Jan-Jun 2026 (CMP-001; national) and flags going-concern uncertainty (CMP-001); its outlet count fell from 715 (31 Dec 2024) to 682 (30 Jun 2026) (CMP-003, CMP-001; national). MAP Boga (MAPB, Starbucks and others) lost Rp121.5 bn in Jan-Jun 2026, wider than the Rp80.2 bn loss a year earlier (CMP-006; national). Champ Resto (ENAK) swung to a nine-month loss in 2025 (CMP-012; national). Pizza Hut's operator (PZZA) stayed profitable but its H1 2026 sales fell 4.54% (CMP-010; national). Fore Coffee (FORE) is the opposite case: revenue +51.7% in H1 2026 (derived) and 377 outlets (CMP-013; national).
- **Foreign entrants.** 16 verified in-window cases, none a documented full exit with a stated reason; four are shrinking. No base rate is given (n<10 documented exits, and the set is not a census).
- **Price ladder.** Item prices are thin and concentrated: 67 numeric prices, three brands hold 83.6%. Latte median 29,000 (n=7, 2 brands), pasta 86,500 (n=20, mixed types), bowl 60,000 (n=15, 2 brands) (price-ladder.csv; Jakarta; 2025 to Oct 2026 captures). No district split exists.
- **Hypotheses.** H1 inconclusive; H2 inconclusive; H3 and H4 gap; H5 partly confirmed; H6 inconclusive; H7 gap. The saturation test is a gap.
- **Implication (Analysis).** District price position cannot be set; the latte placeholder is above every sourced latte; pasta is inside the one Italian brand's range. Field sampling is needed first.

### Limits of the ladder

1. **Approximate prices.** 17 of the 67 numeric rows are given as "sekitar", "-an", "ribuan", Google Maps or ordering-app prices; they are counted at face value (price-points.md DERIVED note).
2. **Brand concentration.** Burgreens supplies 24 of 67 numeric rows (35.8%), Pizza Marzano 18 (26.9%), Fore Coffee 14 (20.9%); the plan's 15% cap is not met. Pasta is 55% Pizza Marzano, coffee 61% Fore, bowls 80% Burgreens, and functional drinks 100% Burgreens. Each all-brand median is a median of a few brands, not of the Jakarta market.
3. **No district split.** Most rows are brand-wide menus. Only a handful carry a place (Senayan City, Senopati/Gunawarman, Kuningan ITC for street pasta); no cell reaches n>=4 for SCBD, Senopati or Kuningan. The plan's per-district statistics cannot be produced.
4. **Italian and bowl prices are thin.** The Italian restaurant benchmark is one brand (Pizza Marzano). The healthy-bowl evidence is one plant-based brand plus Marzano salads. No P25 or P75 is given (brand count below 4).
5. **Tax basis.** Do not mix Burgreens (excl. 10% tax and service) with the media prices in a district median. The "pasta, all types" median mixes kiosk, teppan, plant-based and Italian prices and is not a market median.
6. **Missing.** Starbucks and Common Grounds have no item price; Bastian, Gior and Rosa Osteria appear only as ranges; no delivery-app pairs (gap).

## 6. Saturation test

**GAP.** The plan's saturation test needs district x category counts of qualifying venues and chain outlets within 500 m of candidate streets, with a blind recall check. The input has no such counts: the venue-discovery file was not produced, and the price rows are brand-wide. Only the following partial facts exist: Fore's Jabodetabek network (151 outlets at 31 Dec 2025; CMP-014; Greater Jakarta) and the chain price medians in Section 4. No saturation or white-space verdict is made for SCBD, Senopati or Kuningan.

## 7. Implications for CORSA's positioning and price point

Analysis: these points are interpretation of the data above, not sourced facts.

1. **Price position cannot be set from this data.** No sourced distribution exists by district, and the premium vs "middle" decision (plan decision list) remains open. Do not cite percentiles for any placeholder.
2. **Coffee at 50,000 net is the largest outlier.** Against a sourced latte median of 29,000 and a maximum of 38,000 (2 brands), a 50,000 latte is a clear premium bet. Either the specialty/third-place offer must justify it (to be tested in the field), or coffee should be priced as a traffic driver as the plan suggests if saturation is found.
3. **Pasta looks defensible at the middle of the placeholder range.** The Team B median (about 96,500, derived from the six net prices) is near the Pizza Marzano median (95,000), though tax basis differs and one brand is not a market.
4. **Bowls are unbenchmarked.** One plant-based brand sets the bowl median; the 65,000 placeholder is within its range, but no statement about premium vs middle is supported.
5. **Entrant and operator evidence is directional only.** Mass-tier chains shrink (KFC, Burger King, Starbucks) while experience-tier foreign brands expand (CHAGEE, Paris Baguette, ZUS); with no documented full exit, no survival rate can weight the risk.

### 8.2 Primary-research brief (team, field)

1. **Menu photos in the target districts** (SCBD, Senopati, Kuningan; also Kemang and PIK if time). For each of 8 or more venues per category (pasta, bowl, coffee), photograph the menu, record the date, outlet, price excluding or including 10% PBJT, and the service charge line. Target at least 4 venues per category in each district. Include chains and independents, mall and street.
2. **Field counts.** Walk each district and log all qualifying venues within 500 m of candidate streets (chains named), shuttered units and community programmes, so that H2, H3, H4 and the saturation test can be run.
3. **App price sampling by the team.** On the same day as the menu photo, capture the same item on GoFood and GrabFood (up to 20 items, time-stamped, list price not promo) to measure the delivery uplift against the 15.6% assumption in the model.

## 9. Method and limits

- **Input rule.** Only rows coded VERIFIED-OPENED (original or relay) or CORRECTED-OPENED were used. BLOCKED rows (P3B-17, P3BF-030) were excluded. Relay rows are tier 3 even when the underlying data are filings. Derived values were recomputed from printed figures and are labelled.
- **Dates.** Window cut-off 2024-10-16 per the owner rule; older exits are historical only.
- **Statistics.** From the DERIVED table in price-points.md, recomputed from price-ladder.csv (median = mean of the two middle values when n is even). Statistics with fewer than 4 brands are not market medians.
- **Known limits.** Press-only filings (FAST FY2025, PZZA, Fore guidance relays) are secondary; ENAK is a broker mirror; Starbucks closures and Mixue rest on a single relay each; Greater Jakarta and "Jakarta RSC" are not DKI; price sources are mostly dining-guide articles whose capture dates are 2025, not 2026; no district split; no delivery-app data; verifier acceptance thresholds in plan section 8 apply to the later verification step, not this draft.
