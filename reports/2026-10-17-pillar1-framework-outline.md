# Pillar 1 framework outline: how PESTEL, CAGE and customer profiles fit

Status: DRAFT for Samuel, due 19 Oct 2026. Team A decides the final wording and order. Samuel said the order is guidance, not fixed (meeting 5, 12 Oct).

## Summary

Pillar 1 asks whether the Jakarta entry logic is sound and how CORSA should position itself (scope §6). The scope asks for interpretation, not generic country facts. Three pieces answer three different questions:

- **PESTEL** describes Jakarta's operating context: "how is it there". Political, economic, social, technological, environmental and legal conditions, stated as sourced facts.
- **CAGE** compares Switzerland with Jakarta on cultural, administrative, geographic and economic distance: "what do the differences mean for us". Each gap ends in a consequence for CORSA, for example what transfers and what needs adapting.
- **Customer profiles** say who CORSA serves: which of the scope's groups (young urban professionals, upper-middle-class consumers, fitness communities, creators, students, expats) fit best and how they use cafés and food spaces.

PESTEL is the background, CAGE is the interpretation, profiles are the audience. Findings from all three feed one set of implications for CORSA. Samuel noted that CAGE already covers much of what PESTEL would capture, so PESTEL serves as a summary or addition and does not repeat CAGE.

## Proposed structure for the 22 Oct interim (08:00)

Target: an investor-readable document a non-specialist can follow in about 10 minutes; 3 to 5 bullets per presentation page (Samuel, meeting 5).

1. **Strategic fit assessment.** Is Jakarta a sound first flagship, which assumptions are strong or weak, and how does it compare with Singapore, Dubai and Hong Kong. Source: `reports/2026-10-17-pestel-jakarta.md`, plus `reports/2026-10-17-competitor-map-and-price-ladder.md` and `reports/2026-10-17-demand-economics.md`.
2. **Target customer overview.** Who the most relevant customers are, how strong the fit is, and what triggers purchase. Source: `reports/2026-10-17-customer-target-profiles.md`, plus `reports/2026-10-17-purchase-triggers-and-channels.md`.
3. **Cultural fit considerations.** Which parts of the concept transfer, which need adaptation, which assumptions need local testing (for example halal operations). Source: `reports/2026-10-17-cage-switzerland-jakarta.md`, plus `reports/2026-10-17-regulatory-entry-pathway.md`.
4. **Clear implications for CORSA (scope §6).** Customer, location and validation priorities that feed the joint 90-day roadmap; what to keep, adapt, test. Source: synthesis of sections 1 to 3 and the three reports above.

Evidence base: Plan 1 to 4 reports in `reports/` (listed above), cited through the Evidence and Validation Appendix.
