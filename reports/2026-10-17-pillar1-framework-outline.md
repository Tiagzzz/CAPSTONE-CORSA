## Summary

Pillar 1 asks whether the Jakarta entry logic is sound and how CORSA should position itself (scope §6). It needs interpretation, not generic country facts. Three pieces answer three questions:

- **PESTEL** describes Jakarta's operating context: "how is it there". Sourced facts on political, economic, social, technological, environmental and legal conditions.
- **CAGE** turns the Switzerland-to-Jakarta differences into consequences: "what do the differences mean for us". It builds on Matthieu's CAGE and culture research, now checked claim by claim.
- **Customer profiles** say who CORSA serves: first young urban professionals, then conditionally affluent South Jakarta residents, then run and padel communities and lifestyle café-goers. Students and creators are not launch customers; expats cannot be assessed yet.

PESTEL is the background, CAGE the interpretation, profiles the audience; all three feed one set of implications. Samuel suggested PESTEL as a summary or addition to CAGE, since CAGE already covers much of it, and left the choice to the team (meeting 5).
